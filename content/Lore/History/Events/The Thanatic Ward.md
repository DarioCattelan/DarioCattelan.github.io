# Overview
The Thanatic Ward was a mythical event during the [[Age of the First Bloom]] where [[The Fundamentals]] severed the concept of mortality from their being and placed it behind a construct that has come to be known as [[The Godwall]]. As part of this process, the gods forgot any concept of death as it applies to themselves, and became [[Perpetuals]]. As a result of forgetting the concept of death the gods have also forgotten the exact nature of the Thanatic Ward, and it is unknown if the events of the Thanatic Ward truly took place, or if they are apocryphal.

# Outcomes and Impacts
By separating mortality behind the Godwall, [[The Fundamentals]], perhaps inadvertently, created [[The Afterlife]]. Mortals who perished in [[Kaloshen]] found their [[Breath]] transported to the Underworld, to be reincarnated (as in the [[Nihir Lawall|Elven faith]]) or to continue to a heavenly afterlife (as in [[Truhuggun]]).
