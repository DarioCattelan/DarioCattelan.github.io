# Overview

Wandering the [[Almam Steppe]],  the mythological elven chiefs [[Ciran and Relor]] discovered the [[Alumonium Plowshare]] in the [[Divine Tolls#Toll Locations and Names | Mazarie Toll]]. Taking it as a sign from [[Elenwe The Farmer]] that the land is meant for them, they build the city of [[Cirreal]], and found the Cirrealan Dynasty which will rule the [[Ageless Empire]] for several hundred years.

Although it is unconfirmed by [[The Fundamentals]], folklorists believe that this event marks the beginning of the [[Age of the Elders]].


# Outcomes and Impacts

The [[Ageless Empire]] expands rapidly, conquering most of [[Kaloshen]] and ruling until the beginning of the [[Age of Gentle Waters]].
