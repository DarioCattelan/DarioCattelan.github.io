# Overview

The Edict of Anestrus was a mythological curse placed on [[Elves]] after the rise of the [[Ageless Empire]]. It restricted the population growth of Elves from overwhelming [[Kaloshen]] by magically imposing a biological anestrus period of 128 years.

The Edict of Anestrus was decided following a confluence of [[The Fundamentals]] during the [[Age of the Elders]]. [[Kaloshen]] was newly created, and [[axiomatic mortals]] were just beginning to claim territory and build civilization. The [[Elves]], being [[Perpetuals]], were dying off at a much slower rate than all other mortals, and their population was rapidly overtaking the lands of other races, which angered the other gods.


## The Fable

There came a time after the [[Founding of Cirreal]] where [[Elves]] lived long and prosperous lives. So long and so prosperous were the lives of Elves that of every seed thrown into the fertile soil of [[Kaloshen]], 9 out of every 10 was sewn by an Elven had. Soon every field as far as the gods could see was tended by the Ageless folk[^1], and there rose a powerful envy in the hearts of other mortals.

Many banners were dyed in blood and fields were trampled under the press of armies as the mortals fought over the land.

Seeing so many [[Dwarves]] lost behind [[The Godwall]], [[Dwemeran The Collector]] demanded a remedy to the situation, lest his children be wiped out by the Elven hordes. The gods met in confluence to decide how harmony may be returned to the land. There followed a great negotiation, and by the end, each god provided a piece of their [[Breath]] to [[Elenwe The Farmer]] to resolve the matter.

[[Dwemeran The Collector|Dwemeran]] gave a [[Unfailing Rod|divine fishing rod]], so long that it could reach over [[The Godwall]], its hook so sharp it could snag a soul. With it, he implored, the soul of every elf could be caught, and returned when an elf child is born, so that no new elves be made.

[[The Nomad]] gave a [[Music Box of Delights]], which could tell tales of things yet to come. The box, however, would only sing beautiful melodies, and would only foretell of happy moments. Thus, he suggested, the Elves would not be forewarned of the coming [[The Shifting|Shifting]], and many of their lands would be lost to them.

[[Orthal The Mother]] took one of the [[Septors Seven|scepters of the Elven High Kings]] and broke it in two, forming from it a rattle and a rod. Thus, spoke Orthal, had she separated the roles of family and dynasty in the Elves, so that they might only become parents once every 128 years.

[[Titania The Muse]] suggested that some of the Elves be turned into the other races, so that they might all enjoy a boom of new followers and ideas. To Elenwe she offered the [[Milk of the Moon]], silver milk drawn from the heavens itself that would cause the drinker's form to shift like the phases of the moon.

[[Graedor The Merchant]] wished to give a tonic made of the winter fat of his belly, so filling that the elves who drank it would be made hungerless and have no need for land to farm and rear herds. As he was about to pass it over, he was overcome with such thirst that he drank the tonic himself.

With the gifts of the other [[The Fundamentals|Fundamentals]], [[Elenwe The Farmer]] agreed to relinquish her children's control of [[Kaloshen]], and lay the Edict upon the elves.

# Outcomes and Impacts

The [[Ageless Empire]]'s expansion across Kaloshen was stopped, and the Elven population was no longer growing exponentially without check. Elves since have only been able to reproduce every 128 years after their first period of estrus at the age of 25.
