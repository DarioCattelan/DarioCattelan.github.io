# Overview
The Halfling Diaspora is the period between the destruction of [[Old Netham]] and the founding of [[Netham]], where Halfling ancestral lands were seized by [[The Fundamentals]] and their people scattered. It was a period of great suffering for Halflings, who were treated as second class citizens and a cheap source of labour by other [[axiomatic mortals]].

The Disapora ended with the signing of [[The Freewalk Accords]] following [[Mothers' Heresy]].
