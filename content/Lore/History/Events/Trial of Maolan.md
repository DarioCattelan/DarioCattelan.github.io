# Overview

The Trial of Maolan was a journey undertaken by [[Maolan Antraoir]] and the [[Ice People]] during the [[Age of Gentle Waters]]. Following the destruction of the Royalburrows of the [[Ice People]], the [[the Fundamentals|Fundamental]] [[Dwemeran The Collector]] gave [[Maolan Antraoir]] the task of leading the displaced dwarves South to the newly formed [[Maolan's Mountains|mountain range]] in the center of [[Kaloshen]]. Journeying over many years, Maolan eventually led the [[Ice People]] to found the first Mountainhomes.

## The Deeplings
Within the mountains, Maolan and his followers encounter the [[Deeplings]], cunning creatures who lived in the caverns and chasms of the mountains. Remembering an old wisdom about the nature Deeplings Maolan is able to steal their [[Breath]]. With their Breath gone, the Deeplings pose no threat to the [[Ice People]], who make a home in the mountains.

# Outcomes and Impacts

[[Dwarves]] emigrate from the North of [[Kaloshen]] and settle in [[Maolan's Mountains]].
