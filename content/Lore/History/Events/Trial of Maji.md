# Overview
The Trial of Maji was a journey undertaken by [[Maji of the Mountain]] and the [[Ice People]] during the [[Age of Gentle Waters]]. Following the destruction of the Royalburrows of the [[Ice People]], the [[the Fundamentals|Fundamental]] [[Dwemeran The Collector]] gave Maji the task of leading the displaced dwarves South to the newly formed [[Maji's Mountains|mountain range]] in the center of [[Kaloshen]]. Journeying over many years, Maji eventually led the [[Ice People]] to found the first Mountainhomes.

## The Deeplings
Within the mountains, Maji and their followers encounter the [[Deeplings]], cunning creatures who lived in the caverns and chasms of the mountains. Remembering an old wisdom about the nature Deeplings, Maji was able to steal their [[Breath]]. With their Breath gone, the Deeplings posed no threat to the [[Ice People]], who made a home in the mountains.

# Outcomes and Impacts
[[Dwarves]] emigrate from the North of [[Kaloshen]] and settle in [[Maji's Mountains]].
