# Overview
The Pactum Silvae was an event during the [[Age of Spirits]] where [[Titania The Muse]] made a pact with [[The Nomad]] to hide her people inside [[the Warrens]]. The events of the Pactum Silvae are recorded in the [[Silvaen Tapestry]] and its accompanying historical texts, and have been the subject of numerous poems and plays.

The Pactum Silvae was ultimately a defensive retreat from the [[Corporeal World]] by [[Titania The Muse]] following several calamitous mass [[Spirits#Spirit Possession|Spirit Possessions]] of [[Fey Folk]]. Unable to protect her people from the [[Spirits#Yaji (Demons)|Demons]] that were common during the age, Titania made a plea to [[The Nomad]] for his assistance. Having recently defeated the Great Spirit of the Warrens [[Sonokar Chiri]], the Nomad was still struggling to remove the remaining minor spirits who laid claim to pockets of the dimension. In return for Titania pacifying the spirits, the Nomad agreed her people could occupy the part of the Warrens which sat in the heart of the woods, and that he would never move against this settlement.

Contrary to the Nomad's expectation, Titania did not exile the spirits, but instead bade them to join her people as the first [[Fey Folk#Sylvans|Sylvans]]. Although unexpected, this satisfied the pact, and Hoshi Hall was founded in the Warrens.

# Outcomes and Impacts
 A mass migration of Fey Folk to [[Hoshi Hall]] was seen during the end of the [[Age of Spirits]], which exists as the sole major city of the Fey Folk to this day. The tradition of Sylvans amongst the [[Fey Folk]] continues into the modern age.
