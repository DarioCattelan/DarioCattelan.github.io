# Overview

The Shifting was a geological natural disaster which saw mass earthquakes, the rapid development of [[Maji's Mountains]], the warming of [[Kaloshen]], and the melting of the northern glaciers. [[The Fundamentals]] were aware of the coming disaster, and warned their followers of the danger. After the climate had stabilized, the gods gave direct aid to the [[axiomatic mortals]] in rebuilding civilization.

# Outcomes and Impacts

- The [[Dwarves]] of the North, known as the [[Ice People]], resettle in [[Maji's Mountains]]
- The [[Elves|Elven]] [[Ageless Empire]] splinters into individual city states, never to reform in its full strength
