The Trial of Orisin is a [[Fey Folk|Fey]] Ballet written during the [[Age of the Pillar]] and popularized during the [[Age of Art]]. It is an account of the legend of the witch [[Orisin]] and their invention of the apiary arts. It is one of the oldest cultural dances still performed, and is regularly performed at the [[Royal Conservatory of the Arts]] in [[Hoshi Hall]].

# Synopsis

### Prologue
During the opening act, [[Titania The Muse]] is distraught at the state of her hair. It has grown long and wild, and has turned a dark and sour colour to match her mood. Her three [[Fey Folk#Heralds the Oberons|Oberons]] attempt to brighten her mood with gifts and attention, but get tangled within her hair and choke to death. She calls for servants to attempt to braid her hair, but they too are buried in the tangles and die. Finally, crying on her throne, a [[Fey Folk#Sylvans|Sylvan]] woman comes to her, offering her wisdom, telling her only a comb of the purest gold will master her curls.

## Act 1
In the first act, Titania summons her court of chevaliers to undertake [[The Saltatio Mellis|The Quest for the Golden Comb]]. Four of the knights, Chevaliers Marionette, Renard,  Nainou, and Fleurdelis agree to undertake the quest, and the townsfolk of Hoshi Hall hold a feast in their honour. A fifth character, a humble dancer named [[Orisin|Orisin]] also joins the quest, usually stepping forward from the background dancers of the greater ensemble, but is dismissed by Titania and the chevaliers. Orisin leaves the feast before the four chevaliers, getting a head start on the quest.

## Intermission
There is typically a 20 minute intermission as the feast scene progresses on stage.

## Act 2
The second act opens with Orisin travelling through [[the Warrens]] and come upon a black clad highwayman who robs them at knifepoint. Orisin carries nothing of value but their skills as a dancer, and can only provide the highwayman with a tale. The villain agrees, and Orisin dances a myth of [[The Fundamentals]] creating the world. During their performance, the chevaliers Marionette and Nainou arrive, and mock Orisin, attempting to disrupt their performance. Orisin completes the dance regardless, and the highwayman congratulates them. The highwayman then reveals themselves to be none other than [[The Nomad]], and curses the chevaliers, stealing their [[Breath]] and causing them to forget their quest and wander the Warrens forever. He gives Orisin the bottled Breath of the chevaliers, which is a thick roiling smoke.

## Act 3
By the third act, Orisin has travelled far with no success at finding the Golden Comb, reaching as far as the [[Tankedyr]] city of [[Goadheimr]]. They arrive by ship with the Chevaliers Renard and Fleurdelis, which has been at sea for so long that the food has long ago run out and the crew is starving. Renard suggests that he and Fleurdelis eat Orisin, but the ship reaches port just in time. Orisin and the two knights make their way to the palace of [[Graedor The Merchant]], where a huge feast has been set out. Starving, the two Chevaliers and the crew of the ship devour the food, but Orisin remains behind unwilling to steal from their host. Just then, Graedor returns for [[Truhuggun#Asmenn|Asmenn]], and finding all the food gone, proceeds to eat the Chevalier and crew, sparing Orisin who did not partake of the food and offering them to join in the feast. Following the Asmenn meal, Orisin notices that Graedor has a large stinger stuck in his paw, and offers to remove it. Thankful, Graedor explains he was wounded by a great spirit as he tried to eat her comb of gold. Excitedly, Orisin dons the padded armor of Renard and rushes to find the spirit.

## Act 4
The final act begins with Orisin travelling through fields on the edge of a forest, There they meet a beautiful [[Spirits#Chikara (Great Spirits)|Chikara]] [[Spirits#Zuhachi|Zuhachi]] spirit dressed all in gold and onyx. The spirit reveals herself to be [[Jobachi]], the Great Spirit of Bees. Orisin asks Jobachi if she would come and live with him as his familiar in Hoshi Hall, and if he might borrow her honeycomb for his Queen. Jobachi agrees to live with Orisin if he can dance with her and her children, displaying her sharp stingers and a flurry of rapid footwork. Orisin produces the smokey Breath of the wicked Chevaliers, which slows Jobachi and renders her lethargic, and dances as her partner while protected by Renard's padded armor. Winning the challenge, Orisin returns to Hoshi Hall with Jobachi, who gifts her golden honeycomb to Titania. Eating the honey, Titania's dour mood brightens, her hair turns a beautiful gold hue, and releases Hoshi hall from its brambled clutches.
