# Overview
The Orlin Civil War was a civil war fought between [[Grastus the Free]] and his united [[Three People's Army]] and the Hobgoblin [[Ahltsuki]] Khanate.

# Inciting Events
## Edict of Axiomatic Equality
Upon taking his station as [[Herald of Equality]], [[Grastus the Free]] received an epiphany from [[Orthal The Mother]] on the true nature of the [[The Ordning]]. As a direct result of this epiphany, the [[Orlins|Orlin]] came to believe that all [[The Ordning#Ordning Links|Ordning Links]] were without division, and all of creation was equal within its own Link. Unwilling to relinquish the Ordning based caste system that kept them in power, the ruling [[Ahltsuki]] tribe refused to recognize the epiphany, opposing the supposed will of [[Orthal The Mother]].
## The Pilgrimage of Chains
Enraged by the [[Edict of Axiomatic Equality]], the Khan [[Budan Ahltsuki Khan]] exiled [[Grastus the Free]] and the rest of the Mogbuji priests of Orthal from the Great Palace of [[Cholobataar|Ahltsuger]], forcing them to walk barefoot from [[Cholobataar|Ahltsuger]] to [[Dalgazaad]] bearing a cloak of hooked chains representing the links of [[The Ordning]]. This enraged the faithful Tsagaani tribe, who's warriors ambushed the pilgrimage as they left the city. Returning to [[Cholobataar|Ahltsuger]] with the Tsagaani warriors, Grastus collected the remnants of the Mogbuji tribe and stormed the Grand Palace of Ahltsuger, burning it to the ground and killing [[Budan Ahltsuki Khan]] in the flames.

# Outbreak of the War
When news reached [[Tomorghal Ahltsuki Khan]] in [[Noitanga]] that her mother was dead and that she was Khan, it is said her rage was such that she immediately molted into a spiked and fearsome male carapace. Tomorghal initially had the advantage as they began raising armies from amongst the warrior castes of the Orcs and Hobgoblins in the [[Orlin Plains]] and [[Noitanga]], while Grastus only had access to the far less populous [[Almam Steppe]] to the north of [[Nariindamju]]. Unbeknownst to Tomorghal, however, Grastus accepted volunteers from all castes, including the traditionally non-combative Goblin [[The Suutin|Suutin tribes]], swelling his numbers far beyond what was expected from the region.

# Outcomes and Impacts
With the death of [[Tomorghal Ahltsuki Khan]] and the collected sorcerers of [[Ahltsuki]] tribe, the leaders of the [[Three People's Army]] discarded the Orlin caste system, electing [[Grastus the Free]] as Khan of the Orlin.

Zealous and confident from their defeat of the [[Ahltsuki]], the Zorlogi tribe began raiding the Elven and Dwarven lands of [[Old Netham]], calling for a religious war to end the serfdom of [[Halflings]] suffering under a similar rule to the Orlin caste system. Whether it was Grastus' ultimate intention or not, the Dwarves of [[Dall Cruin]] responded with a declaration of war, incitingthe two decade long conflict known as [[Mothers' Heresy]].
