# Overview
The Saltatio Mellis, or the Quest for the Golden Comb as it is better known in [[Common]], was a legendary event during the [[Age of Witches]] where the witch [[Orisin]] bonded with the Great Spirit of Bees [[Jobachi]], and invented the apiary arts of bee keeping. The primary account of this legend comes from the ballet [[The Trial of Orisin]], dated from the early [[Age of the Pillar]] and popularized during the [[Age of Art]].

# Outcomes and Impacts
- The [[Fey Folk]] of Hoshi Hall have a long tradition of bee keeping, and use honey in all manner of magical arts
- [[Alchemy#Ancient Alchemy|Ancient Alchemy]] is discovered, using honey as a medium for potions and mutagens
