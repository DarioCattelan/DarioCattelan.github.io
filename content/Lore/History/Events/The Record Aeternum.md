# Overview

The Record Aeternum (referred to as the Kitab Khalid in the [[Nihir Lawall]]) was a mythical philosophical poem marrying the concepts of endlessness and natural cycles written during the [[Age of Spirits]] by the Eleven Herald [[Lamaenel Alianquil]] which ended the conflict between [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and the [[Spirits#Chikara (Great Spirits)|Great Spirit]] of Seasons [[Tempus Kami]].  The poem is the foundation of the Elven religious text the [[Nihir Lawall]].

# The Trial of the Seasons


> [!NOTE] Translation
> Here follows a translated text from the original [[Nethanese]], which itself references a primary source written in an Elven dialect of [[Anivara]].
> 
> *Ethelsted of the Galdor, the 112th year since the Wael*

And so it was that many [[Spirits#Kami|Kami]] did march upon the face of the world, and with them came many great winds[^1] which did howl and beset the fair folk of the land. Many a man were those whose [[Breath]] were mingled in the great winds[^2], and the land was filled with the cry of empty cradles, and there came to pass a time of great sorrow. To arms was there a call from three amongst the first mothers and fathers[^3] and a fearsome host was rallied to their summons, but no steel could cut finer than the blood of kinsmen, and the host of the gods were routed.

Foremost amongst the enemy there was a [[Spirits#Taxonomy|Chikara Kami]] whose Breath delivered dolorous wounds to fields and wells and flocks. Such was the injury that I now recount: that the soil became like unto stone so that no plow may till it and all crop were to wither and die, that the wells would grow a thick shell such that the strength of many young warriors were needed to draw a single bucket, that the trees would bleed their leaves in crimson streams to smother the ground, that the air would snap and bite at the skin of beast and man-alike such that they all gathered to the hearth and could raise no hand against their foes, and finally that a blanket of wind and ice that had not been for an age would smother the world. And the Kami whose Breath brought such sorrow was named [[Tempus Kami]], Spirit of Seasons, and he and [[Elenwe The Farmer|the Farmer]] shared much ire and spite.

So it came to pass that [[Elenwe The Farmer|the Queen of the Field]] summoned to her the mortal of her heart[^4], whose name was [[Lamaenel Alianquil]], and was known as Writer of the Long Days and Nights in the tongue of the age, and was greatly known as a mind of much cunning and cleverness. To Lamaenel was given a divine trial of trickery to deceive [[Tempus Kami]] such that the lands would be as they once were, and with great pride did they undertake this task.

Hark, as here I tell you that no trap did defeat the Spirit, for when Lamaenel should spin arcana to bear the cold, [[Tempus Kami]] would part the clouds and blow a stunning heat, or else should they build a great pyre to melt the ice, [[Tempus Kami]] would pull a fortnight of rains from the heavens to douse the flames. Many traps did Lamaenel set, of such great number that I will not list them here, but one may find them in the adjoining fables[^5].

For ten decades did [[Lamaenel Alianquil]] work to trap [[Tempus Kami]], and in that time did they become well known to one another, and a tenderness of the heart arose between them, and it came to pass that Lamaenel and [[Tempus Kami]] did give unto one another a portion of their [[Breath|Breaths]] to keep. And so Lamaenel did find themselves a heart near sundered, for their love was pulled upon by both [[Elenwe The Farmer|the Verdant Queen]] and the Seasons, and by no force could they be driven to wound either one.

And so did [[Lamaenel Alianquil]] recuse themselves for five and twenty days and nights, and in that time did they pour their Breath upon many sheets of velum, and there arose the patterns of their heart into which swirled so perfectly the Seasons and Time such that none could see the seam of their meeting, and it was called the Kitab Khalid[^6]. And many in this time came to the tower of Lamaenel, for they were much beloved, and read did they the poem written upon the velum, and in the Breath of all who beheld it was there a meeting of the gods and the spirit. And to [[Elenwe The Farmer|the Empress]] was brought the poem, as to [[Tempus Kami]], and upon their reading there arose a great joining of Breath, and the nights did grow long and cold, and the trees did bleed their life upon the ground, and the ice did cover the world, but ever did the days of old return.

And to Lamaenel were gifted the longest days and nights, called the alianquil in the old tongue[^7], and there was peace once more between [[Elenwe The Farmer|the Timeless One]] and the Chikara Kami.

# Outcomes and Impacts

- The Seasons are said to have begun as a result of the Record Aeternum
- The record would serve as the first book of the [[Nihir Lawall]]
