# Overview
The Journey of the Steppe is an epic fable set during the [[Age of the Elders]]. It is regarded as one of the seminal [[Orlins|Orlin]] creation myths, and is arguably the most popular tale in the [[Orlins|Orlin]] oral tradition. The myth comes directly from [[Orthal The Mother]], and was first recorded in the [[Age of Orlin]].

# Synopsis
The myth focuses on [[Aya Suutin]], an Orc woman of the [[The Suutin|Suutin tribe]] and [[Herald of Unity]]. Though she is content in her home, [[Orthal The Mother]] asks her to take on a great journey to unify [[Amissa|The Lost Continent]] with [[Kaloshen]]. To accomplish her goal, Orthal gives her [[Unbreakable Thread]] made of horse hair and an [[Immovable Piton]] of chert.

Before she departs, Aya weeps to leave her family and home. Moved by endless compassion, Orthal ties one end of the horsehair thread to the steppe of [[Orlang]]. As Aya travels, the Steppe follows behind her, spreading rich grasslands and gentle plains.

During her journey, Aya encounters many strange folk. Though some prove wary and even outright hostile, Aya overpowers, outwits, or otherwise bests each opponent. At the end of each encounter, Aya shares her skin of milk with her foe, and they become friends thereafter.

Eventually, Aya reaches the coast of [[Kaloshen]], and finds it populated with thinking beasts who live in beautiful jungles. Aya is struck by the majesty of the jungle, and joyously accepts the beasts' invitation to dine together. As Aya travels with her new companions, she is horrified to see that the steppe is razing the verdant forests and destroying the home of these gentle beasts. With a heavy heart, Aya severs the thread connecting her to home, and dines with the jungle beasts, never to return home again.

# Variants
In some versions, [[Aya Suutin]] does not travel alone, but is accompanied by the unnamed [[Herald of Liberty]]. In this variant, when Aya and the Herald of Liberty enter into the jungle, it is Liberty who severs the thread, and Aya returns home to Orlang alone.
