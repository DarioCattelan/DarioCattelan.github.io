# Overview
Mothers' Heresy was a series of overlapping wars waged by the [[Orlins]]  to enforce civil rights and liberties to the [[Halflings]] by providing them their own territory.

# Instigating Factors
## Edict of Axiomatic Equality
*See the [[Edict of Axiomatic Equality]]*
Following  [[Grastus the Free]]'s [[Edict of Axiomatic Equality]], the Orlin underwent [[The Orlin Civil War]] which resulted in the Orlin adopting a religious imperative for the personal liberty of all [[axiomatic mortals]]. 

## The Orlin Civil War
*See [[The Orlin Civil War]].*
Unwilling to dismantle the caste system which maintained their power, the ruling [[Ahltsuki]] tribe of the Orlin attempted to exile and execute Grastus and the rest of the priest in the Mogbuji tribe by forcing them to walk across Kaloshen bearing a cloak of hooked chains. This backfired horrifically, as the faithful Tsagaani tribe liberated Grastus and his kin before they could be killed, leading to the death of the Khan [[Budan Ahltsuki Khan]]. Three years of civil war followed, with a decisive and one sided victory for Grastus' [[Three People's Army]].

## Raids of the Zerlogi Tribes
Following the end of the Orlin Civil War, the Zerlogi tribe began raiding the lands of [[Old Netham]], demanding the freedom of the [[Halflings]] serfs working for the ruling Dwarves and Elves. Failing to persuade their neighbors to release their portion of the ancestral lands of the [[Halflings]], and provide freedom of movement to their national populations, the Orlin invaded.
# Notable Battles

## The Shroud of Buzzards
*c. 187 [[Era of the Five|EF]] - 208 [[Era of the Five|EF]]*

The Orlin Northern Horde lays siege to the cities and fortress of [[Old Netham]], razing farms and seizing any caravans exiting or entering. Flocks of buzzards follow the Horde from city to city as the inhabitants starve to death over the course of years.

## The Burnings
*c. 192 [[Era of the Five|EF]]*

As the Southern Horde crossed [[Orlang]] they suffered constant surprise attack by [[Fey Folk]] calvary. The attackers would charge out of forest portals to [[Hoshi Hall]] and retreat back through the hidden Warrens before the Orlin could mount a response. Driven by desperation, [[Ghoa Anchin Zagashi]], [[Herald of Liberty]], ordered the burning of every major grove and shrubland on the armies path. This enraged the local spirits as well as the [[Fey Folk]], who sent emissaries to the [[Elven Republic]] for aid. The Elves responded by sending the [[Parvaneh Dara Rasu|Herald of Empire]] to accompany the Fey Folks own [[Oberon The Brave]] in meeting Ghoa on the field of battle. Shockingly for the Elves and Fey, Ghoa faced both Heralds themselves, and slew them on the field. [[Hoshi Hall]] capitulated soon after, agreeing to a temporary truce and free passage for the Orlin through [[Orlang]].
## The Siege of Karavanskali
*c. 195 [[Era of the Five|EF]]*

While the Northern Horde traversed the [[Almam Steppe]], the Southern Horde swept across south [[Orlang]] and pushed into [[Audland]], surrounding [[the Kaupangsborg]] city of [[Karavanskali]]. As a trading hub from [[Goadheimr]] to [[Orlang]] and [[Kusisavana]], Karavanskali was well supplied and garrisoned for what the defenders expected to be a long and brutal siege. Shockingly, this would not be the case, as early in morning of the spring of 195 [[Era of the Five|EF]], the northern gate was obliterated from the outside by the [[Ghoa Anchin Zagashi]], the [[Herald of Liberty]]. As Orlin warriors burst into the city the local defenders were rapidly overwhelmed, and by nightfall the city had fallen. Staying within its walls to establish order, the Orlin rested too long upon their laurels, and a relief army out of [[Aho Siebea]] led by [[Dwemer Falakhe Igazichumvi]] caught them by surprised, forcing them to defend the city they had newly conquered. Emboldened by the sight of relief, riots broke out in the city, resulting in the death of several dozen Orlin warriors, including [[Ghoa Anchin Zagashi]] who was beaten to death in a tavern.

The Orlin would defend the city for 3 years, as the attackers were eventually forced to retreat as a virulent plague passed through their camp.
## The Battle of the Pass
*c. 208 [[Era of the Five|EF]]*

The Battle of the Pass was a conflict between the [[Chirian League]] the [[Three People's Army]] in [[Orken Pass]]. It resulted in an Orlin victory, with the annexation of Roadbjur. It was a major defeat for the [[Chirian League]], separating the allied forces of the [[Godsway Trade Alliance]] from the League, and trapping a portion of its army in the southern portion of the Pass.
## Disappearance of Grastus the Free
*c. 208 [[Era of the Five|EF]]*

Following the Battle of the Pass, the Orlin Northern Horde of the [[Three People's Army]] led by [[Grastus the Free]] travelled south through the newly opened Pass to meet with the Southern Horde at the Tankedyr city of  [[Rasteplads]]. In the evening following their victory, the army setup tents in what they believed to be a safely secured campsite. When the morning rose, Grastus the Free was nowhere to be seen. Several eyewitnesses attest that they had seen the Herald enter his tent for the night, and the scouts had not seen anyone enter or leave the camp. Despite these facts, Grastus could not be located, and the commanding officers organized a search for their commander. Delaying their march by two days, the army made a fruitless search of the cliffs and caves of the surrounding area. Many claim that it was this delay that allowed the remnants of the [[Chirian League]]'s defeated army to safely reach [[Rasteplads]].
## The Siege of [[Rasteplads]]
*c. 208 [[Era of the Five|EF]]*

Delayed and dismayed by the disappearance of Grastus the Free, the Three People's Army encircled the fortified city of [[Rasteplads]]. Met by a combined forces of the [[Godsway Trade Alliance]], as well as the rest of the [[Chirian League]] routed from the [[Orken Pass]], the two armies clashed in what would be one of the wars most critical battles. The siege was long and arduous, reaching a critical point when the Orlin breached the northern walls during the third month. Shockingly, the sorcerer [[Warumbe Igazidrako]] rallied the forces of [[Rasteplads]] and managed to close the breach at great cost to the Orlin. Demoralized, bloodied, and on the verge of being overrun by reinforcements on their way from [[Audland]], the [[Three People's Army]] was forced to retreat soon after, splitting once more into Northern and Southern Hordes. The Norther Horde returned up through the Orken Pass, while the Southern Horde continued West into the [[Kusisavana]], raiding deeply into the region relatively unopposed. A large contingent of [[The Olon|Olon]] veterans broke from the Northern Horde to join the Southern Horde, dubbed the Mangari (Buzzard) tribe after the Shroud of Buzzards.
## The Battle of Abu Khasus
*c. 209 [[Era of the Five|EF]]*

Still reeling from the loss of Grastus the Free and their defeat at the Siege of Rasteplads, the Northern Horde of the [[Three People's Army]] marched back into the [[Ardalidin Region]], travelling West to meet the forces of the [[Elven Republic]] in the forest and hills outside [[Abu Khasus]]. Seriously outnumbered by the Orlin, the Elven forces led by the [[Yoharna Lahai Rasul|Herald of Heroes]] used their familiarity with the region to outmaneuver the enemy, trapping their calvary in the bogs and swamps and driving their forces into the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]]. Splintered and broken, the Northern Horde was forced to retreat back East, and would never regain their momentum needed to continue their invasion of the North.
# Outcomes
## [[The Freewalk Accords]]
With both sides severely wounded from over 20 years of war, and with the disappearance of Grastus the Free, the conflict had reached a stalemate. Lacking the strength to retake any of their seized lands, the [[Elven Republic]], [[Dwarven Fiefs]], [[Hoshi Hall]], and the free cities of [[Rasteplads]], [[Straathûs]], [[Goadheimr]], and [[Karavanskali]] officially recognized Orlin possession of the seized territory in return for a cessation of hostilities. The combined people's of Kaloshen were shocked when the Orlin gifted the land back to the empire of [[Netham]], and refused permanent peace without the agreement of emancipation of the [[Halflings|Halfling]] serfs. Ultimately agreeing to the exchange, [[The Freewalk Accords]] were signed, formally ending the Heresy.
