# Overview

The Reditus Domum was an event during the [[Age of Spirits]]  where [[Spirits]] began manifesting and corporealizing in [[Kaloshen]]. Having never encountered encountered [[Spirits]] before, the [[axiomatic mortals]] had no defense or knowledge of how to appease [[Spirits]], and many Great spirits wreaked havoc on civilization. [[The Fundamentals]] were divided on how to treat [[Spirits]], with [[Titania The Muse|The Muse]] and [[Dwemeran The Collector|The Collector]] seeing their arrival as a fascinating delight, [[The Nomad]], [[Graedor The Merchant|The Merchant]], and [[Elenwe The Farmer|The Farmer]] seeing Spirits as a challenge to their power, bringing them in direct conflict with [[Orthal The Mother|The Mother]] who wished to protect and nurture the Spirits. As a result, guidance from the gods to mortals was inconsistent and often dangerous. 

# Outcomes and Impacts

## Conflict within the Gods
[[The Via Aperitur]] and the Reditus Domum broke the unstable unity of [[The Fundamentals]] seen during the [[Age of Gentle Waters]]. Divided both on how to treat the newly arrived [[Spirits]], as well as who should have domain over [[the Warrens]], the gods once again challenged each other for supremacy of [[Kaloshen]]. Going so far as to even sabotage each other's positions of power, and opposed by several Great Spirits, [[The Fundamentals]] had, for the first time, encountered a true threat to their dominance over the [[Corporeal World]].
