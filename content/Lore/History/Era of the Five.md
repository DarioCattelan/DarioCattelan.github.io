The Era of the Five is the start of the [[Perpetual Calendar]], with the year 1 starting at the end of [[The Wæl]] and the advent of the [[The Wæl#Memorum Obscura|Memoram Obscura]]. It is so named as [[The Fundamentals|the Six Fundamentals]] became five after the imprisonment of [[The Nomad]]. Dates are either labeled ES (Era of the Six) or EF (Era of the Five). There is no year 0.
