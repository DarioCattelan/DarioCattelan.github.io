# Overview
The Pillaneum was the eponymous legendary tower built during the [[Age of the Pillar]] in the city of [[Forbannoda]]. Formed of fulgurite from the [[Divine Tolls|Budsan Toll]], the Pillaneum was a massive tower of translucent glass that rose so high it wounded the sky. The Pillaneum was constructed by [[Tumultuarii]], who used the tower as a headquarters while they developed their dark and heretical [[The Breath of the Mind|Breath of the Mind]].

# Destruction
The Pillaneum was destroyed shortly before [[The Wæl]] during the battle between [[Marrowgorn Ahltsuki]] and [[Alfred of Netham]].

# Arcana
The Pillaneum was said to have a terrible natural [[Arcana]] that made it, and all that occurred within, invisible to the divine. Additionally, despite being made of glass, the Pillaneum was flexible and strong, extending so high as to "wound the sky with its spire".

## Curse of Forbannoda
When the Pillaneum was destroyed a large piece of the sky was sundered and fell crashing to the eath, crushing [[Forbannoda]] and turning its people to stone. An oppressive arcana hangs about the ruined city, and none may enter by decree of [[The Fundamentals]].
