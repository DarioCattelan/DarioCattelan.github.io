# Overview
Forbannoda was a [[Tallmen]] city in the empire of [[Old Netham]] that sat in the [[Divine Tolls|Budsan Tool]] in the middle of the [[Budsan Desert]]. It was the headquarters of the [[Tumultuarii]] and home of the [[Pillaneum]]. The city suffered greatly during [[The Wæl]] and was abandoned.
