The Bauyokhan Empire was a unified [[Orlins|Orlin]] empire lead by Hobgoblins during the [[Age of Orlin]]. During the height of its power, the Bauyokhan Empire possessed territory ranging from the start of the [[Placeholder ardanil straight]], to the edge of the [[Southern Steppe]].

The Bauyokhan Empire was close allies with [[Old Netham]].
