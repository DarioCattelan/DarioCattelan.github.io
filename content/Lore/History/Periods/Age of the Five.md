# Overview
The Age of the Five is the modern age and start of the [[Perpetual Calendar]]. The Age of the Five began with a calamity known as [[The Wæl]], where [[The Nomad]] betrayed the other [[The Fundamentals|Fundamentals]] and the gods were reduced from six to five. Following the devastating impacts of [[The Wæl]] and the confusion of the resulting [[The Memorum Obscura|Memorum Obscura]], the civilizations of Kaloshen struggled to recover, often exploiting the now diasporic [[Halflings]] as a source of cheap labour.

In response to the tumultuous times, the independent mercantile cities led by [[Tankedyr]] merchant princes form [[the Kaupangsborg]], a powerful alliance which does much to stabilize trade.

Enlightened by divine epiphany, [[Grastus the Free]] pushes revolutionary changes for the mortal rights of the [[Orlins|Orlin]], sparking the [[The Orlin Civil War]]. The zealous tide of Grastus' ideals eventually lead his armies to sweep across the world in [[Mothers' Heresy]]. [[Mothers' Heresy]] ends with [[The Freewalk Accords]], ending [[The Halfling Diaspora]] and refounding [[Netham]].

- [[The Memorum Obscura]] takes effect, ultimately erasing all definitive history before this age
- ~10 EF [[Hobbsborg|Roadbjur]] and [[Rasteplads]] form a military alliance
- ~20 EF The roads and [[the Warrens|Warrens]] become dangerous to travel, with several large caravans disappearing.
- 24 EF The first [[Undead]] appears in [[Kaloshen]]
- 89 EF [[Reith Habass Rasul]] seizes [[Straathûs]] in a naval assault
- 94 EF [[Karavanskali]] joins the alliance with [[Rasteplads]] and [[Hobbsborg|Roadbjur]], forming [[the Kaupangsborg]]
- 121 EF A [[Tankedyr]] merchant guild called the [[Cult of Fasting]] uses [[Spirits#Alcohol Binding|Alcohol Binding]] to force [[Spirits#Akumi (Devils)|Devils]] into pacts to produce gold and other rich resources. the practice spreads rapidly, stabilizing the economy of [[the Kaupangsborg]]
- 157 EF A group of [[Tankedyr]] warlocks infiltrate the [[Hoshi Hall#The Backwoods|The Backwoods]] of [[Hoshi Hall]] and try to awaken [[Sonokar Chiri]]. The ritual goes wrong, and several new species of birds burst from the forest of [[Kaloshen]]
- 184 EF [[The Orlin Civil War]] begins when [[Grastus the Free]] becomes Herald of [[Orthal The Mother]] and throws down the Orlin caste system
- 187 EF [[Mothers' Heresy]], a conflict between the [[Orlins|Orlin]] and [[Halflings]] against the [[Elves]], [[Dwarves]], [[Tankedyr]], and [[Fey Folk]], begins as the Orlin continue their goal of world wide freedom following the civil war
- 193 EF [[Straathûs]] is purchased from the [[Elven Republic]] by the Merchant Prince [[Jorunn Sokkoldottir]] and joins [[the Kaupangsborg]]
- 207 EF [[Grastus the Free]] disappears after the [[Mothers' Heresy#The Battle of the Pass|Battle of the Pass]]
- 209 EF [[Mothers' Heresy]] ends with [[The Freewalk Accords]] and the refounding of [[Netham]]
- 210 EF [[Goadheimr]] joins [[the Kaupangsborg]], officially recognizing its statehood. The rest of the modern city states soon follow.
- 219 EF  [[The Norwâld Contract]] is signed establishing [[Norwâld|the Norwâld Reserve]]
- 223 EF [[Lady Belladonna]] writes [[The Cry of the Sacred Sparrow]]
