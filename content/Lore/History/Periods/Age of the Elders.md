# Overview
The Age of the Elders is mythical age where [[Elves]] vastly outnumbered the other [[axiomatic mortals]] and ruled over [[Kaloshen]] uncontested. Many [[Elvish]] tales depict this period as a time of stewardship and parental guardianship as wise Elvish kings and queens protected over the land and its people from the primeval beasts of [[Kaloshen]]. Other cultures, such as the oral tradition of [[Halflings]], tell stories of authoritarian tyrants and despots during this age, with themes of imprisonment and enchantment.

By the end of the Age of Elders, the other [[The Fundamentals|Fundamentals]] convince [[Elenwe The Farmer]] that the [[Perpetuals]] are conquering too much of the world, and as a result [[Elenwe The Farmer]] greatly increases the [[Elves#Reproduction|anestrus reproductive period of elves]].
