# Overview
The Age of Spirits is a mythical period when [[the Warrens]] opened for the first time and [[Spirits]] began manifesting in [[Kaloshen]]. Great spirits corporealize and wreak havoc upon [[axiomatic mortals]] as they possess mortals and disrupt nature. Seasons begin for the first time. The unity of [[the Fundamentals]] is disrupted as they vie for control over [[the Warrens]] and the power of [[Spirits]]. The [[Fey Folk]] are sequestered away from the [[Corporeal World]], founding [[Hoshi Hall]] in the Warrens.
