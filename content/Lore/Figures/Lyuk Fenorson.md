# Overview
Lyuk Fenorson was a grey wolf presenting [[Tankedyr]] who served as the first territorial warden of the [[Norwâld Territory Administration]] and founder of its original mercenary company [[The Waldbist]]. They are famous for their defense of [[Norwâld]] during a raid by [[Orlins|Orlin Warriors]] in 219 [[Era of the Five|EF]].
