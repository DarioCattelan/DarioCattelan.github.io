
Nalu Ipsin (or "Shadowlocks" as they are known in [[Common]]) is a mythological figure from the [[Fable of the Weaver]]. In the fable, Nalu is distraught at the death of their beloved, and travels all the way to [[The Godwall]] to try to retrieve them. Upon reaching the wall, Nalu slices it open only to discover the souls on the other side are not ready to return, and materialize as monsters. Nalu attempts to correct their mistake by sewing up the seam in the wall using their fine hair, and is trapped between life and death for eternity within the Godwall.

Nalu is a cautionary figure in Elven mythology, and has become synonymous with someone undertaking foolhardy or poorly planned action.
