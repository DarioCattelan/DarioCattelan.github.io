# Overview
The Blasphemer was a mythical [[Heralds|Herald]] associated with the traitorous god [[The Nomad]], alongside [[The Highwayman]]. The station was destroyed during [[The Wæl]].

# Folklore
The Blasphemer is a villain in several mythical tales originating in the [[Age of Orlin]], [[Age of the Pillar]], and [[Age of the Five]]. It is not clear if the legends of the Blasphemer are based on an actual historical person(s).

In the tales, the Blasphemer would weave magic into lies, sowing discord and betrayal amongst the faithful.
