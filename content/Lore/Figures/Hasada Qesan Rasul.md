# Overview
Hasada Quesan Rasul is the matriarch of the [[Clan Rasul]] and leader of the [[Prodiji Shah]], serving as the Herald of the Harvest. She is the second eldest member of clan Rasul, after [[Hevlon Ibara Rasul]], her great-granduncle. Despite only being the second eldest Shah of Clan Rasul, Hasada serves as its head ever since Hevlon's diagnosis with [[Sariqa Syndrome]].

# Heraldic Duties and Powers
As Herald of the Harvest, Hasada is charged with the cycles of toil and bounty. She appears in several fables dating back to the [[Age of the Pillar]] where hard work and diligence lead to prosperity, frequently to detriment of the lazy and dishonorable. It is said that Hasada ensures that all people of [[Kaloshen]] reap that which they sow, be it fortune or folly. She is celebrated alongside [[Elenwe The Farmer|Elenwe]] in the homes of farmers even beyond the limits of the [[Elven Republic]], and her figure sometimes adorns shrines to [[Spirits]] of fields and gardens.

She is also the bringer of just vengeance and misfortune, and tales featuring Hasada rarely end well for the foolhardy or careless.
