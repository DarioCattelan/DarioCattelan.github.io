# Overview
Oberon the Brave, born Riddlesticks, was a [[Fey Folk]] knight and [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Titania The Muse]]. He was a member of the [[Chivalric Orders of the Fey#The Order of the King's Valets|Order of the King's Valets]] before becoming gaining favor for his bravery with [[Titania The Muse]] and becoming an [[Oberon]].

Oberon the Brave led the forces of [[Hoshi Hall]] during [[Mothers' Heresy]]. He was killed by the [[Herald of Liberty]] [[Ghoa Anchin Zagashi]] when he led a company of knights to oppose [[Mothers' Heresy#The Burnings|the Burnings]].

# Heraldic Duties and Powers
Oberon the Brave was a mighty warrior who could wield [[The Breath of Devotion]]. He could inspire heroism in those around him, and could heal the wounded. He had the ability to hone his Breath to a sharpened edge about his blade, and smite enemies.
