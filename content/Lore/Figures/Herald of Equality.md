# Overview
The Herald of Equality is one of the three [[Heralds|Heraldic]] stations of [[Orthal The Mother]] and the [[Orlins|Orlin]]. Prior to the [[Edict of Axiomatic Equality]] and [[The Orlin Civil War]], the position was known as the Herald of Royalty and was traditionally associated with the crown of Khan held by the [[Ahltsuki]] tribe.


> [!Local] GM Notes
> The position is not truly vacant, as [[Grastus the Free]] still wanders the world

# Heraldic Duties and Powers
The Herald of Equality seeks freedom, justice, and peace between all peoples. They ensure that the laws of the Gods apply to all beings, be they prince or popper. Their voice is commanding and irresistible, and any harm that befalls them is felt too by their attacker. Courage and belonging can be felt in their presence, and  if they wish, even the sun will descend to meet them eye to eye.
