# Overview
Ithun Amarus Rasul is a [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and member of the [[Prodiji Shah]], holding the station of the Herald of Politics. He is a famed orator and politically outspoken member of the [[Clan Rasul]], and serves as the party whip in [[Elves#Projidi|political arm of the clan]]. Ithun often serves as chief diplomat for [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and the [[Elven Republic]].

# Heraldic Duties and Powers
The Herald of Politics is a far reaching station that governs the cyclical rise and fall of ideologies and governments. In historical literature, the Herald of Politics is often an omen presaging revolution or the dismantling of empire. In academia, however, the station is frequently depicted as a symbol of enlightenment and novel thought.

In [[Lady Belladonna]]'s epic poem *[[The Cry of the Sacred Sparrow]]* [[Grastus the Free]] is visited by the Herald of Politics and the [[Yoharna Lahai Rasul|Herald of Heroes]] before being appointed as Herald of [[Orthal The Mother]], possibly prompting Grastus to begin [[Mothers' Heresy]].

Ithun often travels in the company of the [[Velash Bedora Rasul|Herald of Fashion]], a station occupied by his sister [[Velash Bedora Rasul]].
