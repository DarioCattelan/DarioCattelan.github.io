# Overview
Grastus the Free, nee Grastus Takhil Mogbuji, is an Orlin-Hobgoblin [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Orthal The Mother]] who enacted the [[Edict of Axiomatic Equality]] and led the Orlin in [[Mothers' Heresy]]. Grastus holds the station of the [[Herald of Equality]], though is often erroneously associated with the station of [[Herald of Liberty]] due to his involvement in [[Mothers' Heresy]].
# Biography
## Early Life
Grastus was born into a high ranking priestly caste of [[The Suutin]] Mogbuji tribe in [[Cholobataar]]. Not much is known of his life before 184 EF, where he was appointed [[Herald of Equality]] by [[Orthal The Mother]] at the age of 16.

## Later Life
Ever since the signing of  [[The Freewalk Accords]], Grastus the Free has lived in exile on a small island in the middle of the [[Divine Tolls|Mazarie Toll]] in the [[Almam Steppe]].
