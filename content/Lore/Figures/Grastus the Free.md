# Overview
Grastus the Free, nee Grastus Takhil Mogbuji, was an Orlin-Hobgoblin [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Orthal The Mother]] who enacted the [[Edict of Axiomatic Equality]] and led the [[Three People's Army]] in [[The Orlin Civil War]] and [[Mothers' Heresy]]. Grastus held the station of the [[Herald of Equality]], though he is often erroneously associated with the station of [[Herald of Liberty]] due to his involvement in [[Mothers' Heresy]].
# Biography
## Early Life
Grastus was born into a high ranking priestly caste of [[The Suutin]] Mogbuji tribe in [[Cholobataar]]. Not much is known of his life before 184 EF, where he was appointed [[Herald of Equality]] by [[Orthal The Mother]] at the age of 16.

## orlin Civil War
Soon after his appointment, Grastus received a epiphany from [[Orthal The Mother]], leading him to declare the [[Edict of Axiomatic Equality]]. This edict was violently rebuked by the ruling [[Ahltsuki]] Khanate, and Grastus was exiled to die walking from [[Cholobataar|Ahltsuger]] to [[Dalgazaad]] in a cloak of barbed chains. What the Khanate did not count on, however, was the zealous faith of the local [[The Olon|Olon tribes]] of the region, who saw the death of a Herald as a terrible blasphemy. Defying the Khanate, the Tsagaani tribe saved Grastus and slew the soldiers of the Khan, sparking [[The Orlin Civil War]].

Grastus led the rebellion to victory over the [[Ahltsuki]] Khanate, deposing the tribe. Grastus was made Khan of the Orlin, and leveraged his rule to realizing his dream of dismantling the caste system that had controlled Orlin life for centuries.

## Mother's heresy
Zealous and confident from their defeat of the [[Ahltsuki]], Orlin tribes began raiding the Elven and Dwarven lands of [[Old Netham]], calling for a religious war to end the serfdom of [[Halflings]] suffering under a similar rule to the Orlin caste system. Whether it was Grastus' ultimate intention or not, the Dwarves of [[Dall Cruin]] responded with a declaration of war, inciting the two decade long conflict known as [[Mothers' Heresy]].

Once again at war, the now 19 year old Grastus led the [[Three People's Army]] in a devastatingly effective two front push to the North and South of Logres, seizing huge portions of the [[Almam Steppe]] and [[Orlin Plains]]. Grastus' unique tactics and natural talent for logistics and millitary stratagem swept through opposing armies fielded by [[Elven Republic]], [[Hoshi Hall]], and [[Dwarven Fiefs]] to confront him in the steppe. Seeing that Grastus could not be defeated in open battle, the opposing forces retreated to their cities, hunkering down for what would be 20 years of sieges.

## Disappearance
Grastus the Free disappeared during an assault on the city of [[Rasteplads]]. His sudden loss was a huge blow to the unity and strategic vision of the [[Three People's Army]], and quickly lead to the end of [[Mothers' Heresy]].
