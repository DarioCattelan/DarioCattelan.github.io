# Overview
Tauret Djoser Rasul was an elven sorcerer who founded the [[Elves#The Majilis|Majilis]] during the [[Age of Witches]] and started the [[Clan Rasul|Rasul]] dynasty of [[Heralds]]. They were the first member of the [[Prodiji Shah]], granted the title of Herald of Time by [[Elenwe The Farmer]]. Although the bylaws of the Majilis have changed and expanded since the senate's inception, the system and structure of governance is fundamentally unchanged from Tauret's conception.
