# Overview
Ciran and Relor are the mythical founders of [[Cirreal]] and [[Ageless Empire]]. They are said to be the progenitors of the empires first dynasty, and  [[Clan Rasul]] claims they are their descendants.

# Death
Ciran and Relor are said to have perished with the fall of the City of [[Cirreal]] during [[The Shifting]].
