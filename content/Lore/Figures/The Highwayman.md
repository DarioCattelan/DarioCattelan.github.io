# Overview
The Highwayman was a mythical [[Heralds|Herald]] associated with the traitorous god [[The Nomad]], alongside [[The Blasphemer]]. The station was destroyed during [[The Wæl]].

# Folklore
The Highwayman is a villain in several mythical tales originating in the [[Age of Orlin]], [[Age of the Pillar]], and [[Age of the Five]]. It is not clear if the legends of the Highwayman are based on an actual historical person(s).

In the tales, the Highwayman would cheat and steal from the faithful and defenseless along the roads and tollways.
