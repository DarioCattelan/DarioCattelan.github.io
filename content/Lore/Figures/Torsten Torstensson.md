# Overview
Torsten Tostensson, better know by his alias the Dread Pirate Torstensson, was a [[Tankedyr]] pirate who seized several wealthy vessels from the [[Elven Republic]] during the [[Age of Art]], running them aground and founding the city of [[Straathûs]].
