# Overview
Hannu Egilson, known as Hannu Kindlyone in [[Netham]], was a [[Tankedyr]] witch during the early [[Age of the Five]]. They are known for reforming the [[Coven of the Wobbly Witch]] following the events of [[The Wæl]], with a focus on providing comfort to [[Spirits#Alcohol Binding|bound Spirits]].

# Biography
## Early Life
Hannu was orphaned by the events of [[The Wæl]]. In their late teenage years, they joined a group of adventurers, who came to be known as the new Coven of the Wobbly Witch.
