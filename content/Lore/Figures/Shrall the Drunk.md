# Overview
Shrall the Drunk was a [[Tankedyr]] brewer during the [[Age of Witches]]. She was a brewer who with the aid of her familiar Elddis discovered the means of distilling alcoholic spirits strong enough to permit [[Spirits#Alcohol Binding|Alcohol Binding]], an event known as the [[The Ritus Ieiunii|The Rite of Fasting]]. Prior to her discovery, oral tradition claims that alcohol did not leave one hungover. Following her discovery, Shrall founded the [[Coven of the Wobbly Witch]] to help bind dangerous [[Spirits]].
# Biography
## Early Life
During her early life, Shrall was said to be a brewer of some repute in a town in modern day [[Audland]]. Her business was successful enough that she owned her own fields, and was invited to the [[Truhuggun#Asmenn|Asmenn]] of [[Graedor The Merchant]].

## Adulthood
Following the [[The Ritus Ieiunii|The Rite of Fasting]], Shrall was sought by many folks across Audland to help them bind dangerous spirits. Shrall eventually collected a following of arcane practitioners dedicated to the binding of spirits called the [[Coven of the Wobbly Witch]].
## Later Life
During the end of their life, Shrall was said to have regretted the "damage they had done to the spiritual world" by binding so many mighty spirits. They encouraged members of the Coven to only use binding as a last resort, and that they should aid bound spirits in their transition to life in the [[Corporeal World]]. This was not well received, and Shrall was ejected from her coven, though her ideals would eventually be adopted by the coven's refounding during the [[Age of the Five]].

## Death
Shrall is said to have died in 608 [[Era of the Five|ES]] at approximately 60 years of age due to complications from alcoholism.
