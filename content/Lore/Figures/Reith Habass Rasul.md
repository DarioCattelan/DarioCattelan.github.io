# Overview
Reith Habass Rasul is a [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and member of the [[Prodiji Shah]], holding the station of the Herald of Tides. He is the eldest living child of [[Hevlon Ibara Rasul]] (the official patriarch of [[Clan Rasul]]), but has a relatively minor role in Clan politics due to the nature of his heraldic duties, and the diminishing influence of Hevlon following his retirement from the Shah.

Reith also serves as the [[Tide Praetor]] of the [[Elven Republic]]'s navy.
# Heraldic Duties and Powers
The Herald of the Tides is a mighty position that is said to hold sway over the ebb and flow of the forces of nature. In the *The Legend of the Great Wave* the Herald of Tides was able to push back the ocean out of the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]] so that refugees could flee to [[Abu Khasus]] during [[Mothers' Heresy]], and is considered the patron herald of the city.

The duties of the Herald of Tides is not restricted to bodies of water, as they are said to have command over the changing of the winds, as well as the migration of birds and beasts.

In legend, the Herald of Tides is often accompanied by the [[Ashiel Rasul|Herald of the Moon]].
