# Overview
Tempus Kami was [[Spirits#Chikara (Great Spirits)|Chikara (Great Spirit)]] [[Spirits#Kami|Kami (of the world)]] who controlled the changing of the seasons during the [[Age of Spirits]]. They are the main antagonist of [[The Record Aeternum#The Trial of the Seasons|the Trial of the Seasons]] in which they are a direct rival to [[Elenwe The Farmer]].

# Conflict with the Fundamentals
Tempus Kami's ability to change the seasons brought famine and death to many mortals in Kaloshen who had never needed to contend with winter or rainy seasons. This brought Tempus Kami in direct conflict with [[The Fundamentals]], in particular [[Elenwe The Farmer]] who's domain at the time included time and endlessness. In opposition to Tempus Kami the gods fielded an army to decorporealize him, but were ultimately unable to sustain their army during the winter. In response, [[Elenwe The Farmer]] tasked her Herald [[Lamaenel Alianquil|Liara Solstice]] to defeat Tempus Kami.

# Romance with Liara Solstice
Liara was unable to defeat Tempus Kami by strength of arms or trickery, and eventually the spirit and the Herald fell in love. Unwilling to force the spirit to decorporealize, and unwilling to betray her goddess, Liara wrote a poem for the twin love she felt for Tempus Kami and Elenwe. The poem was so widely applauded that it changed the perception of the seasons, and [[Elenwe The Farmer]] agreed to include the cycles of the world into her domain.

# Ultimate Fate
After the adoption of seasonality into the domains of the Fundamentals, it is not know what happened to Tempus Kami. Several notable covens believe that as the seasons were adopted as a normal and venerated aspect of life, and with the advent of spirit binding and pacts in the [[Age of Witches]], Tempus Kami was appeased and eventually decorporealize and demanifested.
