# Overview
 Aldgift Heralda was a [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[The Nomad]] during the [[Age of Spirits]]. They are feature in the [[The Victoria Viae#Fable of Sonokar Chiri|Fable of Sonokar]] as a protagonist who seeks a story that will send [[Sonokar Chiri]] into a deep sleep. In the fable Aldgift is herself enchanted by the story, and the narrative continues with her daughter, [[Eadgift Wicca]] as the protagonist. Aldgift's ultimate fate is unknown, but it is implied at the end of the Fable of Sonokar that she awakens.
