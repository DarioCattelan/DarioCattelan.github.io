# Overview
The Herald of Liberty is one of the three [[Heralds|Heraldic]] stations of [[Orthal The Mother]] and the [[Orlins|Orlin]]. It is the station of Goblins, and the bearer of the station is always a Goblin. The station is currently held by [[Brego Shurdarga Olontsohki]].

# Heraldic Duties and Powers

The Herald of Liberty is said to watch over and ensure freedom for that which is meant to be free, and imprisonment for that which is not. Doorways and locks bend to their will, and every promise is a prayer to their station. No roof can keep the Herald of Liberty from the open sky, and no force in this world can break their grip once it is set.

The Herald of Liberty keeps no hearth nor home, travelling the open plains where the horizon kisses the sky. Beasts rejoice at their passing, and builders pray for their blessing before breaking ground on dungeon or oubliette. Whoa be them who thoughtlessly renders judgement on the liberty of others, for they shall be themselves host to the Herald of Liberty, and may find their [[Breath]] freed in turn.

# History
## Journey of the Steppe
It is said in the oral tradition of [[The Olon]] that the Herald of Liberty was asked to accompany the [[Herald of Unity]] on their fabled [[Journey of the Steppe]], and that it was Liberty, not Unity, that severed the thread connecting [[Amissa]] to [[Kaloshen]].

## The Orlin Civil War
During [[The Orlin Civil War]], the Herald of Liberty did not participate on either side at the request of [[Orthal The Mother]], however, they were present at the defeat of the last [[Ahltsuki]] khan.

## Mother's Heresy
The Herald of Liberty [[Ghoa Anchin Zagashi]] was the leader of [[Three People's Army#The Southern horde|Southern horde]] during [[Mothers' Heresy]]. They were responsible for orchestrating [[Mothers' Heresy#The Burnings|the Burnings]] and the successful [[Mothers' Heresy#The Siege of Karavanskali|Siege of Karavanskali]]. They were directly responsible for the death of two [[Heralds]], the [[Parvaneh Dara Rasu|Herald of Empire]] and [[Oberon The Brave]] on the field of battle. [[Ghoa Anchin Zagashi]] was killed in [[Karavanskali]] shortly after the siege, and was replaced a few years after by [[Brego Shurdarga Olontsohki]].
