# Overview
Sonokar Chiri was a [[Spirits#Chikara (Great Spirits)|Chikara (Great Spirit)]] [[Spirits#Chiri|Chiri (of the land)]] who guarded [[the Warrens]] during the [[Age of Spirits]]. He was an adversary of [[The Fundamentals]], and was challenged by [[The Nomad]] for control of the Warrens. He is the central antagonist of [[The Victoria Viae#Fable of Sonokar Chiri|the Fable of Sonokar Chiri]].

# Powers
Sonokar Chiri was said to be "of endless length" and possessing of impressive strength and fortitude. He had a scaled hide that was resistant to harm, sharp teeth, and could produce fire from his eyes. In [[The Victoria Viae#Fable of Sonokar Chiri|the Fable of Sonokar Chiri]] it is suggested that he has some degree of truthsense, but could not discern lies of omission or partial truths.

# Death
According to legend, Sonokar Chiri was petrified by [[Eadgift Wicca]] when she told him the story of his own demise. It is said that Sonokar Chiri's [[Breath]] is imprisoned within the [[Sonokar Stone]], which prevents his manifesting in the [[Corporeal World]].
