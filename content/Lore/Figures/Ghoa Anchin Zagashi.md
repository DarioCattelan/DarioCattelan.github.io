# Overview
Ghoa Anchin Zagashi was a Goblin [[Orlins|Orlin]] of the Zagashi [[The Olon|Olon tribe]] who held the station of the [[Herald of Liberty]] during [[The Orlin Civil War]] and the beginning of [[Mothers' Heresy]]. They led the Southern Horde of the [[Three People's Army]], and defeated the [[Parvaneh Dara Rasu|Herald of Empire]] and [[Oberon The Brave]]. Ghoa was killed by [[Tankedyr]] peasants in [[Karavanskali]] shortly after the [[Mothers' Heresy#The Siege of Karavanskali|The Siege of Karavanskali]].

# Biography

## The Orlin Civil War
When [[Grastus the Free]] was gifted an epiphany and led a rebellion against the ruling [[Ahltsuki]] Khanate, it is said [[Orthal The Mother]] summoned Ghoa Zagashi to her side to observe the conflict. When the last [[Ahltsuki]] Khan was killed in battle, the skalds claim Ghoa split their skull horizontally at the cap and placed the tyrant's skullplate at the feet of [[Grastus the Free]] as a crown.

## Mothers Heresy and Death
When [[The Orlin Civil War]] spilled into [[Audland]] and the [[Elven Republic]], Ghoa was appointed commander of the Southerne Horde of the [[Three People's Army]]. Ghoa led the Orlin conquest through [[Orlang]] and was commanding the army during [[Mothers' Heresy#The Burnings|The Burnings]] and [[Mothers' Heresy#The Siege of Karavanskali|The Siege of Karavanskali]].

### Death at Karavanskali
Marching the Southern horde up to [[Karavanskali]], Ghoa conquered the city in 195 [[Era of the Five|EF]] during [[Mothers' Heresy#The Siege of Karavanskali|The Siege of Karavanskali]]. Shortly thereafter, the city erupted into riots, and Ghoa was beaten to death by [[Tankedyr]] peasants in a tavern.
