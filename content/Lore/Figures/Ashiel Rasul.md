# Overview
Ashiel Rasul is a Herald of [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and member of the [[Prodiji Shah]], serving as Herald of the Moon. She Is the third eldest member of [[Clan Rasul]] after [[Hevlon Ibara Rasul]] and [[Hasada Qesan Rasul]].

# Heraldic Duties and Powers
As Herald of the Moon, Ashiel is charged with the cycles of the heavens. It is said that she convinces the Moon to change its face each month, chases the stars across the sky, and rouses the sun from its slumber each dawn. Ashiel rarely appears in fables as a protagonist compared to the rest of the Shah, with the notable exception of the highly popular *The Moon and the Mole* where she prevents [[The Gadian Ri]] from stealing away the immortality of the [[Elves]] as tax for [[Dwemeran The Collector]]. In legend, Ashiel is frequently depicted as a companion of the [[Reith Habass Rasul|Herald of the Tides]].
