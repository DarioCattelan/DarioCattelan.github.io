# Overview
Aramia Miska Rasul is a member of [[Clan Rasul]] and is the Herald of Spring of the [[Prodiji Shah]]. Unlike the other members of the Shah, she is not an elder member of Clan Rasul, but was chosen by [[Elenwe The Farmer]] as the Herald of Spring due to her vitality and youth. Aramia was appointed Prodiji Shah in the year 89 [[Era of the Five|ES]] at the age of 25, and is the youngest member of the Shah by several centuries.

# Heraldic Duties and Powers
As Herald of Spring, Aramia is said to govern the renewal of life. Legend holds that she travels across all of [[Kaloshen]] thawing the Winter snows in the North, and quieting the monsoons in the South. She is said to have been present at the birth of great kings and queens of the past age, and her touch can cleanse curses and afflictions.

Aramia has a unique cultural position in [[Elves|Elven]] society in that her blessing of a pregnancy removes the stigma of childbearing during an elf's initial cycle of fertility.
