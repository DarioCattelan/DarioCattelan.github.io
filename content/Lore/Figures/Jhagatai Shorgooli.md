# Overview
Jhagatai Shorloogi is an [[Orlins|Orlin]] statesman and current [[Herald of Unity]].

> [!Local] GM Notes
> ## Appearance
> Heavily mottled carapace of dark brown almost black chitine with pale red edges
> Large lips, flat nose, bent back, watery eyes of pale orange. Thick forearms, once broad chest now flabby.
> Typically wears a deel in shades of orange and red, trimmed in furs.

# Biography
## Early Life
Born in [[Dalgazaad]] to a wealthy family in the Shorloogi Suutin tribe, Jhagatai was raised to serve as a priest and advisor to the [[Ahltsuki]] tribe. in 178 [[Era of the Five|EF]] at the age of 33, Jhagatai was appointed the [[Herald of Unity]] by [[Orthal The Mother]] following the accidental death of his predecessor. For 6 years, Jhagatai was head of [[The Ordning]]
