# Overview
Kuldrus Hagrutha was the ruler of the Dwemer [[Dall Cruin]] fief from 433 [[Era of the Five|ES]] until their death in 221 [[Era of the Five|ES]]. They are most known for their discovery of [[Alchemy#Chemical Alchemy|Chemical Alchemy]], and are dubbed the Dwemer of Alchemy.
# Biography
## Early Life

## Discovery of Chemical Alchemy
Following the discovery of [[Candescent Sand]] Kuldrus became fascinated with its unique chemical properties and its interactions with honey, developing several new uses for the substance in [[Alchemy#Ancient Alchemy|Ancient Alchemy]]. This led them to explore other volatile powders, delving deeply into the mines of [[Maolan's Mountains]] for saltpeter, phosphorous, sulfur, and other reagents.

Their experimentations came to a head in 278 [[Era of the Five|ES]] when they first published the processes which would form the foundation of Chemical Alchemy at the age of 216. Their methodology would only be included into [[Thastus' Innovative Method]] posthumously in the year 211 [[Era of the Five|ES]], on the 10 year anniversary of their passing.

## Later Life

## Death
Kuldrus died of an unknown illness in 221 [[Era of the Five|ES]] at the age of 273. Alchemists believe they may have been suffering from long term poisoning as a result of their lifelong experimentation with chemical reagents.
