# Overview
Warumbe Igazidrako is a scholar and member of the [[Igazidrako Dynasty]] and 12th in line for the Throne of the [[Aho Siebea]]. Like many in the dynasty, Warumbe is a mage who has an innate affinity for [[The Breath of the World]]. He is most known for defending a breach in the walls of the city of [[Rasteplads]] during [[Mothers' Heresy]], earning him the moniker "the Dragon of Rasteplads".
