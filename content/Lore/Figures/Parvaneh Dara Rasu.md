# Overview
Parvaneh Dara Rasul was a Herald of [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and member of the [[Prodiji Shah]], serving as the Herald of Empire. She was killed in battle by [[Ghoa Anchin Zagashi]] during [[Mothers' Heresy]] in 192 [[Era of the Five|EF]].

# Heraldic Duties and Powers
The Herald of Empire was said to govern the rise and fall of new empires, laws, and cultures. Walls of stone would rise at their summons, cities welcomed their approach, and their voice could be heard by a crowd even at a whisper. Their presence was said to inspire loyalty and obedience, and they could compel legions with merely a look from their eye.
# Biography

## Death
Parvaneh was killed in battle when she flew out to face the [[Three People's Army#The Southern horde|Southern Horde]] to oppose their destruction of ancient forests during [[Mothers' Heresy#The Burnings|the Burnings]]. She was met by the [[Orlins|Orlin]] [[Herald of Liberty]], [[Ghoa Anchin Zagashi]], who smote both Parvaneh and [[Oberon The Brave]] in a shocking two-on-one duel.
