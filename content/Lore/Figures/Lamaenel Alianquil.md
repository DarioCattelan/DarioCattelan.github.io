# Overview
Lamaenel Alianquil was a poet and [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Elenwe The Farmer]] during the [[Age of Spirits]]. She is an important spiritual figure in the [[Nihir Lawall]] religion having written [[The Record Aeternum]] which is the basis of the Nihir Lawall's first book. Lamaenel Alianquil is the protagonist of the fable [[The Record Aeternum#The Trial of the Seasons|the Trial of the Seasons]].

# The Trial of the Seasons
*See [[The Record Aeternum]]*

Following the events of [[the Reditus Domum]], many Great [[Spirits]] were in direct conflict with [[the Fundamentals]] for dominion over [[Kaloshen]]. Lamaenel Alianquil was given the task of trapping the Great Spirit [[Tempus Kami]], Spirit of the Seasons. After a hundred years of attempts, they became enamored with the Spirit and shared their [[Breath]]. Unable to bring themselves to harm [[Tempus Kami]] or [[Elenwe The Farmer]], Lamaenel wrote a poem of exultation to the cycles of nature and time. The work was so influential it brought a peaceful resolution[^1] to the conflict of [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and [[Tempus Kami]], with [[Elenwe The Farmer|Elenwe]] adopting cyclical nature into their domain, bringing about the seasons.

# Death
It is unknown when Lamaenel Alianquil died or their resting place. Some versions of the Trial of the Seasons have Lamaenel pass away after writing the [[The Record Aeternum|Kitab Khalid]], while others tell that she lived a long life with [[Tempus Kami]].
