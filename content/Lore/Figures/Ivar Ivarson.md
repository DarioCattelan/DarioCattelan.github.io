# Overview
Ivar Ivarson, sometimes called Ivar the Hungry, was a [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Graedor The Merchant]] during the final years of the [[Age of Orlin]]. He was a world renown cook, and held the station of the Herald of Hunger. Ivar is remembered as a compassionate philanthropist, who used his powers as Herald to feed the dispossessed and the needy during the tumultuous age.

Ivar is also the original owner of [[Cornucopia]].
