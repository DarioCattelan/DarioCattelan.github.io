# Overview
Velash Bedora Rasul is a [[Heralds|Herald]] of [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and member of the [[Prodiji Shah]], holding the station of the Herald of Fashion. Velash is a widely acclaimed polymath artist, with celebrated paintings, tapestries, novels, plays, and culinary dishes, and has started numerous fashion trends. Several pieces of her work are included in [[Titania The Muse]]'s royal gallery in the [[Royal Conservatory of the Arts]] of [[Hoshi Hall]].

# Heraldic Duties and Powers
The Herald of Fashion is said to govern the ebb and flow of fads, trends, tastes, and public opinion. Often considered a lesser station, the Herald of Fashion is regardless present in dozens of light hearted fables and tales from the [[Age of the Pillar]] as a cunning heroine who bests evil sorcerers and tyrannical goblins by tricking them into acting against their self-interests.

Velash's influence over the culture of the [[Elven Republic]] cannot be overstated, and is considered a benign magical consequence of her station. Even unassuming public comments by the Herald of Fashion can lead to huge swings in public perception and associated economic trends.

Velash is frequently accompanied by her brother, the [[Ithun Amarus Rasul|Herald of Politics]].
