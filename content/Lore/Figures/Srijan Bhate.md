# Overview
Srijan Bhate is a young but wealthy member of [[The Cult of Mazarie]] and Personal Secretary to the [[Elenwe The Farmer|High Tari]]. Despite not being born to one of the elven noble clans, he has amassed enough wealth and position to be highly influential in the politics of the [[Elven Republic]].

# Biography
## Early Life
Srijan Bhate was born to middle class labourers in the [[Elven Province of Yarahn]] in the year 172 [[Era of the Five|EF]]. When [[Mothers' Heresy]] reach the [[Elven Republic]], he left his home at the age of 15 to join the legions mustering in defense of the land. Srijan's company was wiped out in the [[Mothers' Heresy#The Shroud of Buzzards|Shroud of Buzzards]], leaving him as one of the few survivors.

Upon recovering from his wounds, Srijan volunteered to join [[The Summer Company]], becoming a non-commissioned officer. Srijan comported himself with honour, and was with the company during its victory at [[Mothers' Heresy#The Battle of Abu Khasus|Abu Khasus]].

Retiring as a decorated veteran, Srijan was gifted a plot of land in the [[Elven Province of Canub]] under [[Clan Tajik]].

## Adulthood
Sometime during his time in Canub, Srijan became a devoted member of [[The Cult of Mazarie]], eventually owning several small temples. He was a member of [[Clan Tajik]]'s close advisors, having served under [[Suvan Roshanir Tajik]], becoming her chief treasurer and master of correspondence.

Srijan travelled to [[Henil-nesut]] in 223 [[Era of the Five|EF]], where his skills caught the eye of [[Elenwe The Farmer]]. In the Winter of 223, he was appointed Personal Secretary to the [[Elves#High Tari|High Tari]].
