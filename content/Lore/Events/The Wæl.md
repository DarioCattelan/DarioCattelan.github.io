
The Wæl (wahl) was a calamity that nearly caused the extinction of [[Tallmen]] 200 years ago. The nature of the Wæl has been forgotten by all [[axiomatic mortals]], but involves the betrayal of [[The Nomad]] to the other [[The Fundamentals|Fundamentals]], and the destroying of [[Old Netham]].

# Memorum Obscura
The Memorum Obscura is a curse placed upon [[axiomatic mortals]] during the events of the Wael. The Memorum Obscura magically muddles the memories of [[Perpetuals]], causing their recollections from before the Wæl to be inconsistent and unreliable. It is believed that the Memorum Obscura is the work of [[The Nomad]].
