The Dwarven Fiefs, sometimes called the Dwarven Kingdoms, are a series of independent theocratic monarchies ruled by [[Dwarves#Dwemer rule and fiefs|Dwemers]] (dwarven kings and queens). They are the ancestral land of the [[Dwarves]].

# law
Laws in the Dwarven Fiefs are ultimately enforced by the Dwemer, but are conceived by followers of [[Esoteric Timism]] who interpret [[The Codex|The Codex of TIM]] for ethical codes of conduct.
