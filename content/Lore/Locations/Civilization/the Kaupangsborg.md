The Kaupangsborg are the collection of mercantile city states founded in 94 [[Era of the Five|EF]] by [[Tankedyr]] [[Tankedyr#The Pashas and Princes|Pashas]]. Several of the city states which are located in the Tankedyr homeland of [[Audland]], though not all. Although the Kaupangsborg are independent, the holy city of [[Goadheimr]] is often described as the "first amongst equals" of the Kaupangsborg.

The Kaupangsborg are ruled via mercantile oligarchy led by [[Tankedyr#Merchant Councils|merchant councils]]. Many cities of the Kaupangsborg are seated in territories claimed by other [[axiomatic mortals]], but maintain a fierce independence by virtue of their economic and military power.

# History
- The mercantile oligarchy of the Kaupangsborg first started appearing near the end of the [[Age of the Pillar]] with the repossession of the immense wealth of the [[Coven of the Wobbly Witch]] by Tankedyr merchants.
- Shortly after the fall of the Pillar and the devastation of [[The Wæl]], the merchant princes of [[Rasteplads]] and [[Hobbsborg|Roadbjur]] banded together to defend their territories against marauding armies
- In the year 94 [[Era of the Five|EF]] [[Karavanskali]] joins the alliance, officially creating [[the Kaupangsborg]]
- 193 EF [[Straathûs]] is purchased from the [[Elven Republic]] by the Merchant Prince [[Jorunn Sokkoldottir]] and joins [[the Kaupangsborg]]
- 210 EF [[Goadheimr]] joins [[the Kaupangsborg]], officially recognizing its statehood. The rest of the modern city states soon follow.
