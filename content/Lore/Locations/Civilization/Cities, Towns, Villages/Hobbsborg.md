
Hobbsborg is the largest city in [[Netham]], and sits at the edge of the [[Orken Pass]]. 

# History
It is believed the city now known as Hobbsborg was founded nearly 6 hundred years ago by [[Humans]] in the lost nation of [[Old Netham]]. The original name of the city has been lost, with some scholars thinking it bore the titular name of Netham, other claiming it was called Clayof after the [[Spirits#Chiri|Chiri]] of [[Orken Pass]].

During [[The Wæl]] over 200 years ago, the Hobbsborg of antiquity was seized by the forces of [[Graedor The Merchant]], and renamed Roadbjur after the large ochre arch which ringed its gate. Over a hundred years ago, the [[Tankedyr#The Pashas and Princes|Merchant Princes]] of the Tankedyr formed the coalition of city states known as [[the Kaupangsborg]], with Roadbjur as one of its founding members.

During [[Mothers' Heresy]], Roadbjur was conquered by the [[Three People's Army]] during [[Mothers' Heresy#The Battle of the Pass|the Battle of the Pass]]. Following the signing of [[The Freewalk Accords]], Roadbjur and its surrounding lands were returned to the [[Halflings]] as restitution for the destruction of [[Old Netham]].
