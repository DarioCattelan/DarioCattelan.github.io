# Overview
Bubasta is a large city in the [[Elven Republic]] located on the norther coast of the [[Placeholder ardanil straight]]. It is a major trade port, and was the major naval power in the North until the founding of [[Straathûs]].

Bubasta was the capital of the [[Elven Republic]] until the invasion by [[Orlins|Orlin]] and [[Tallmen]] in 474 [[Era of the Five|ES]], at which point the capital was transferred to [[Henil-nesut]].
