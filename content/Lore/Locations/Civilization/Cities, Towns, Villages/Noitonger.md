# Overview
Noitonger is a city in [[The Orlin Communes]] to the north east of the [[Orlin Plains]] at the edge of [[Noitanga]]. It is the ancestral home of the Zogii [[The Suutin|Suutin]] tribe. The name Noitonger literally translates as "Wet Home" in [[Common]].
