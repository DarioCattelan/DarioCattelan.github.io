Straathûs is a maritime city of [[the Kaupangsborg]] which sits within the [[Ardalidin Region]] overlooking the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]]. Straathûs is within the borders of the [[Elven Republic]], and maintains independence through economic and military force. It is a potent naval power and collects tariffs for vessels travelling through the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]].

Straathûs has changed hands many times throughout its history and has been an independent city state, part of the [[Bauyokhan Empire]], the [[Elven Republic]], and [[the Kaupangsborg]]. Despite its many theoretical political upheavals, Straathûs has in reality been highly stable, with a robust mercantile oligarchy holding the true reigns of power since its founding.

The eight titanic [[Gardener Trees]] dominate Straathûs' skyline, giving the city an endless supply of wood, but preventing the growth of any edible crops.

Straathûs has a long tradition of whaling, and is a major source of whale biproducts for the [[Ardalidin Region]].

# Districts
Straathus is divided into 7 districts.

## South Torsten
The gentrified half of the founding district of the city, South Torsten is a walled district where the oldest families in Straathûs keep their manors. A district of rich manicured lawns stretching before palatial manors and expensive shops, people from the Scabs or North Torsten are rarely welcomed here by the professional private guards. [[The Captains of the Coast]] have their ancestral Edgeholm manors carved into the cliffs of the Edgefjord along this district, and the secrets held within the warehouses of the Captain's Docks have driven many a thief mad with curiosity. A massive cathedral to [[Graedor The Merchant]] looms over the mansions in this district, inviting the rich to come and make tithe to the [[Truhuggun#The Chantry of Truhuggun|The Chantry of Truhuggun]].

## North Torsten
The working class heart of the city, North Torsten and South Torsten were once a single district before the wall was erected some 300 years ago. Dirty, claustrophobic, and exciting, the North District wears the age of the city for all to see in its crooked buildings and narrow roads.

Whales are butchered and drained along its shores, and the Northern Market is the best place in the city to get fresh fish. Thousands attend Asmenn at the massive Cathedral in the heart of the district.

The bustling and vibrant Entertainer's Guildhall draws even the merchant princes to the district, and North Torsten is home to the Huggun Rest House, Straathûs' famous tavern and inn.

## Pied-a-Lac
Also called the Faerie District, even from afar a visitor to Pied-a-Lac is made awestruck by the colossal [[Gardener Trees]] that blanket the district in perpetual shade. The uneven cobblestone streets of this district drain sewage openly down to the ocean as even the smallest roots of the Gardener Trees destroy any subterranean infrastructure. Many spirit shrines surrounded by small patches of harsh and prickly greenery struggle against the hunger of the Trees across this district. Lumber mills sit at the base of each tree, sawing like termites at the endlessly regrowing branches, and all manner of building materials can be purchased at the Faerie Market to the East.

The district holds a certain air of mystery to it, as Fey Folk build their homes in hidden alleys, burrowed hills, and along secret canals all across this district. The original elven shrine to [[Strahatra-Chiri]] sits along the coast, all but forgotten by the elves in favour of the grand temples in the Elven District.

## Elven District
Thoughtfully and carefully developed from the centuries long vision of a few old clans, the Elven District has a strong architectural identity that calls to mind a miniature [[Bubasta]]. Petrified trees, killed suddenly during the growth of the [[Gardener Trees]], but remade into something timeless and beautiful, stand proud next to minarets and arches of stunningly tiled stone.
Many private guilds and businesses build their halls in this district, vying for proximity to the Pasha's new mint, the Hafkorn. Anyone can rent space at the Pilgrim Docks here, and ships come and go at all times of day. The naval academy guildhall to the north ever supplies a fresh throng of young, brash officers breathing a life into the district's taverns and bars. Like Pied-a-lac, many households in this district have small spirit shrines surrounded by patchy weeds and twisted shrubs.

## Landhaals
Young and growing, Landhaals is the newest, and perhaps richest, of the districts of Straathûs. Many younger siblings and affluent cousins of the greater [[the Kaupangsborg|Kaupangsborg]] nobility have made their way to Straathûs and settled in this district to form a distinct class of "nouveau riche" princes, competing for power with the well entrenched [[The Captains of the Coast|Captains of the Coast]]. Reminders of the [[Elven Republic]]'s annex of this city, impressive elven style manors, built for the officers of the [[Reith Habass Rasul|Herald of Tides]], still stand in this district, many still occupied by their original owners.

The Dawnhall, the meeting place of [[The Captains of the Coast]] and the major guilds, sits upon Bilge Beach to the north of the district, where [[Torsten Torstenson]]'s infamous vessel, the Damnation, ran aground. Textiles and cotton from the western farms, as well as an exotic assortment of imported goods and sea pearls, can be bought at the Merchant's Market in this district.

The Heraldic Temple of Truhuggun stands at the top of a hill in the center of the district, where monks brew wines and the ill are treated. In juxtaposition to the south stands the massive lighthouse fortress of Thunderwatch where elven warriors still train.

## The Scabs
Packed with buildings and people, the Scabs are nearly a city unto themselves. Evolving a completely unique mix of Orlin, Tankedyr, Elven, and Feyfolk architecture, the Scabs look like a collage of three nations joined together at the seams with [[Orlins#Crud|crud]]. Most of the servants, labourers, sailors, soldiers, and guards of the merchant guilds live within the Orlin hive houses of this district, crossing the many bridges of the edgefjord each morning to spread across the city.

No major guilds build their halls in the Scabs, and as a result investment in the district infrastructure is poor. Despite this fact, the district is kept clean and maintained by the community and its temples, and locals like to say the Scabs is the poorest district in coin, but richest in heart.

Two large temples are the corner stone of the Scabs community: the imposing Bauyokhan Public Temple, and the Suutin Heraldhome. Both institutions are well funded by [[The Orlin Communes]], and they pour heaps of gold back into the community.

## Conqueror's District
Once the seat of power for Orlin conquerors, the eponymous district now holds a middle class of tradespeople, artisans, diplomats, and retired mercenaries. The Scars, Straathus' largest mercenary guild, makes its hall here, and the streets have a military order to them. Buildings are larger and more crud-based than the Scabs, but still distinctly Straathusian in their collage of cultures.

Herders and shepherds bring their stock to Veteran's Plaza for slaughter and sale, and [[The Olon|Olon]] who work the farms to the north can be seen riding along the streets. The Conqueror's Hive, an ancient castle from before [[The Wæl]], sits on the edge of the Scabs, now serving as a treasury and vault for hire. [[The Orlin Communes]] have a foreign office here, one of the only of its kind across the world.

# Landmarks
1. **Lake Straathûs:** A dingy lagoon, polluted by the sewage and runoff of the city. [[Strahatra-Chiri]], a powerful spirit, is said to slumber within.
2. **Fjord Edgeholm:** A beautiful fjord splitting Torsten from the Scabs, crisscrossed by the roots of the [[Gardener Trees]]
3. **Edgeholms:** Five beautiful manor houses built into the cliffs for [[The Captains of the Coast]] by [[Fey Folk]] explorers.
4. **Roots of the Gardener Trees:** The roots of the massive [[Gardener Trees]]. They drain the greenery from the land and make docking along the North-west of Lake Straathûs impossible
5. **Our Lord of Silken Rest Cathedral:** A lavish, gothic cathedral that preaches damnation and charity to the wealthy of Straathus
6. **Captain's Docks:** Private docks of the pasha and merchant princes of Straathûs. The warehouses along the docks are well guarded and filled with treasures.
7. **Dawnhall and Bilge Beach:** The "townhall" of Straathus where the merchant princes and affluent guild masters meet to govern the city. Built from the Damnation, the once feared ship of the dread pirate [[Torsten Torstenson]].
8. **Shrine to Sato-Chiri:** The Shrine to [[Sato-Chiri]], spirit of cliffs where sits Landhaals. Serves as public park and graveyard to the city's elite.
9. **Heraldic Temple of Truhuggun:** Temple to the Herald's of [[Graedor The Merchant]]. A peaceful abbey where monks brew wine and treat the ill.
10. **Merchant's Market:** A textiles market where the cotton fields of the west and the merchants of the Pilgrim's docks bring their goods.
11. **Rasul Plaza:** A plaza of surrounded by expensive artisans and shops, where a statue of [[Reith Habass Rasul]] stands in bronze.
12. **Thunderwatch:** A Heraldic temple fortress of the Elves that serves as monastery and lighthouse. Dedicated to the [[Reith Habass Rasul|Herald of Tides]], cultists of the Mazarie train in the martial arts here.
13. **Pilgrim Docks:** Docks, owned by [[The Captains of the Coast]], but rentable to the public, where most foreign trade arrives in the city.
14. **Guild Docks:** Private docks where the wealthiest guilds keep their vessels.
15. **Hafkorn:** A grand treasury and mint commissioned shortly after the purchase of Straathûs by [[Jorunn Sokkoldottir]]. Fiercely guarded by the [[Pearl Keepers Guild]].
16. **Mazamiret:** A stunning grand mosque dedicated to [[Elenwe The Farmer]] where followers of [[Nihir Lawall]] partake in public [[Elves#The Mutacurir|mutacurir]] while the many faithful of [[Truhuggun]] take their [[Truhuggun#Asmenn|asmenn]]. Elves can also engage in [[Elves#Cilaf|cilaf]] here with [[The Cult of Mazarie]].
17. **Straathûs Naval Academy:** A private academy teaching naval warfare.
18. **Heraldic Temple of the Shah:** A Heraldic temple of the Elves dedicated to the [[Parvaneh Dara Rasu|Herald of Empire]]. Cultists of the Mazarie bear funerary garb here, mourning the Herald of Empire, and teach the Histories of the Gods in oral tradition. Arms and armor of great quality are smithed here, and can be purchased.
19. **The [[Gardener Trees]]:** Titanic trees grown magically in a single night from a miracle of [[Elenwe The Farmer]]. Their branches ever regrow, and all manner of magical animals live in the canopies. Lumber guilds saw incessantly at their branches, and their roots rob the soil of the ability to grow anything edible.
20. **Midday Revelry Brewers:** A jolly, well known public house, selling ale of the highest quality, and hard spirits used for binding. Frequented by [[Fey Folk]] and officers of the Naval Academy.
21. **[[Coven of the Guilty Gardener]] Guildhall:** The guildhall of the witches coven dedicated to protecting the natural landscape of Straathûs. Often vilified by [[The Captains of the Coast]], but tolerated for their maintenance of the [[Gardener Trees]]. A small library of spirit and herblore is kept here.
22. **Faerie Market:** A market where the lumbermills sell the materials they harvest from the [[Gardener Trees]], including the rare magical fruit or pelt.
23. **[[The Waldbist|Wâldbist]] Guild Hall:** Guildhall of the rangers of [[Norwâld]].
24. **Shrine to [[Strahatra-Chiri]]:** A tori gate made of pine trees grown in a twisting, covered tunnel. The original shrine of the native elven population worshiping the spirit of the lagoon, Strahatra-Chiri, from which Straathus gets its name.
25. **Tanner Bay:** A strip between two brackish canals where the tanneries, soap makers, foundries, and other caustic businesses reside, dumping their runoff into the canals and lake.
26. **Entertainer's Guild:** A plaza of several competing entertainer guilds' halls and their theaters. A unique form of nori theater has its roots here.
27. **Whaling Docks and Harbour Master:** The docks where whales are brought to butcher and have their oils rendered. The Habour Master has their office here.
28. **Huggun Rest House:** A large, multi-story tavern and inn, famous in the region for their hospitality. Many local bards vie for the chance to perform here.
29. **First Asmenn Cathedral:** The first temple to [[Graedor The Merchant|Graedor]] built in this city, hundreds flock to this massive cathedral every week to take [[Truhuggun#Asmenn|asmenn]].
30. **Northern Market:** A bustling fish market and center of the North Torsten community. Mongers, hawkers, buskers, vendors, and criers compete in a raucous celebration of trade.
31. **Communes Foreign Office:** A public house for Orlin kept by representatives of [[The Orlin Communes]]. Part of a fresh diplomatic approach by the Communes to foster unity between the [[axiomatic mortals]].
32. **Veteran's Plaza:** A public plaza where husbands come bring their stock to auction. The best butcheries of the city ring the plaza.
33. **Olon Heraldhome:** A [[Orlins#The Herald Home|heraldhome]] for [[The Olon|Olon tribals]] as well as friends and family. Houses a meaty perpetual stew.
34. **Scars Guildhall:** Guildhall of [[The Scars]], a mighty mercenary troop often hired to defend the city in times of war.
35. **Conqueror's Hive:** A once fearsome keep, now a vault for hire by the very rich and distrustful.
36. **Bauyokhan Public Temple:** A large communal temple to Orthal that serves as community center and heart of the Scabs. An ancient sect of Orlin monks keep this hall with much knowledge on the tending and care of animals.
37. **Suutin Heraldhome:** A [[Orlins#The Herald Home|heraldhome]] for [[The Suutin|Suutin tribals]] as well as friends and family. Houses a fishy perpetual stew.
38. **Lake Gurkhan:** A small natural lake at the top of the edgefjord. Named after Gurkhan, a mythical Orlin boy who nearly caught a fish of gold from this lake. Holds a fishing competition each year.
39. **Farmsteads:** Farms and ranches. Edible crops do not grow for miles around Straathûs, instead, the farms to the west grow cotton and other textiles plants, while the ranches to the east rear livestock.
40. **Godtsep Lake:** A collection of warm lakes in the vague shape of a pawprint, where it is said [[Graedor The Merchant]] once tread in the [[Age of the First Bloom|Age of Creation]].
# Factions
- **[[The Captains of the Coast]]:** The descendants of the original founders and merchant princes of Straathûs who have held the true political power in the city for centuries
- **Landhaalians:** "Nouveau riche" merchants, near equal in wealth to the Captains of the Coast, but the lesser in influence.
- **[[The Scars]]:** A powerful mercenary company that once conquered the city, now serves the city's princes. Defends the city during times of war.
- **[[Coven of the Guilty Gardener]]:** A coven of witches dedicated to protecting the natural landscape of Straathûs. Often vilified by [[The Captains of the Coast]], but tolerated for their maintenance of the [[Gardener Trees]].
- **The [[Pearl Keepers Guild]]:** A powerful merchant guild that runs the Hafkorn mint.
- **The Tide Captains:** Elder elves who were once officers of the [[Tide Praetor]], willingly left behind when the Herald left the city.

# History
- 283 ES: Straathûs was founded in the [[Age of Art]] by the [[Torsten Torstensson|Dread Pirate Torsten]] when he seized several Elven ships blocking the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]] and ran them aground, choosing the bay for its protective fjord and the massive trees that would serve as excellent shipbuilding.
- Torsten seizes a small settled shrine of elven priests that worship the great spirit of the lake.
- The captains and crew of Torsten's armada form a coalition of captains dubbed The Captains of the Coast. The coalition has no codified system of law, relying on the honour of the sea, and a written law develops slowly over time.
- The elven sailors captured by the pirates have their wealth stolen and merge with the native elven population. Many elves stay in the the settlement to protect the spirit of the lake and their claim to the land.
- 241 ES: The elven heralds arrive, and denounce the treatment of the land by the pirates. The natives elves move their settlement to the other side of lake Straathus
- 207 ES: [[Elenwe The Farmer]] convinces [[Graedor The Merchant]] to strike Straathus with a famine. All bounties of the sea pulled from the coast of Straathus becomes poisonous. The labourers of the city riot and strike against the Captains of the Coast. Orlin labourers from the encroaching [[Bauyokhan Empire]] arrive in Straathus to take up the labour of the dead and dying. Priests of Graedor arrive on mission to give comfort and witness during the famine.
- 168 ES: [[Fey Folk]] explorers arrive from [[Hoshi Hall]] on a quest to replenish the ancient trees of Straathus. In return for land, they build into the cliffs of Fjord Edgeholm a series of beautiful mansions for the Captains of the Coast.
- 132 ES: The gentry of Straathus build a wall between north and south Torstentown, further stratifying the class divide. The poorer, North Torstentown begins to swell, while the rich southern portion becomes deeply isolated.
- 118 ES: [[Orlins|Orlin]] mercenaries turn on the Merchant Princes of [[Straathûs]], seizing the city for themselves. Orlins from across the elven realms arrive to Straathus, bringing with them worship of Orthal the Mother.
- 82 ES: Elven priests and Fey druids mix traditions of magic, forming a coven of witches known as the [[Coven of the Guilty Gardener]]. The coven works to sabotage the exploitation of Straathus' natural resources, in particular, Lake Straathus and the great spirit therein. Orlin population booms as priests of Orthal encourage family values.
- 48 ES: The artistic traditions of the Tankedyr and the Fey Folk mix in North Torsten, creating a new style of art featuring masks. An entertainment guild forms, and builds up North Torsten into a burgeoning entertainment district.
- 6 EF: The new money of South Torsten seek fresh earth to build their family homes. No longer feeling the sense of tradition and dynasty after [[The Memorum Obscura]], they take to the craggy hills of The Rise, creating sprawling mansions at exorbitant cost.
- 42 EF: After 200 years of divine magic, [[Elenwe The Farmer]] turns her gaze towards the ritual of the Pied a Lac, granting them her favour. 8 titanic trees, whose trunk cannot be girdled by less than four score elves and more, immediately sprout in the Stumps. The roots of the trees skewer through the end Fjord Edgeholm, creating a canyon criss-crossed with thick wooden roots. The Captains of the Coast seize the land, and begin to profit from the rapid magical growth of the forest, and its strange and divine creatures.
- 89 EF: Offended by Straathus' commercialization of Elenwes miracle, [[Reith Habass Rasul]] seizes [[Straathûs]] in a naval assault
- 144 EF: The [[Coven of the Guilty Gardener]] angered by the failure of any rulers to cleanse Lake Straathus, decide to awaken the spirits of the Lake. Angry, malformed spirits of polluted fish and plants roam the streets. The [[Coven of the Guilty Gardener]] make pacts with the demons of the deep, cursing the acestors of the Captains of the Coast. The Eleven Republic sends warriors to the city to fight the demons.
- 165 EF: The [[Reith Habass Rasul|Herald of Tides]] asks the sea to make him a bridge to the southern island. Upon its rock he builds a great lighthouse temple named Thunderwatch. Officers of the Elven armada build their homes in Landhalls, next to the original old money families of the Tankedyr.
- 193 EF [[Straathûs]] is purchased from the [[Elven Republic]] by the Merchant Prince [[Jorunn Sokkoldottir]] and joins [[the Kaupangsborg]]
- 201 EF: [[Jorunn Sokkoldottir]] constructs a the Hafkorn, a grand mint, and mints a new currency to solidify the cities joining with [[the Kaupangsborg]]. The coin depicts the Thunderwatch on one side, and the Godstep on the other, honouring the history and people of the city. A new guild, the [[Pearl Keepers Guild]], forms.
