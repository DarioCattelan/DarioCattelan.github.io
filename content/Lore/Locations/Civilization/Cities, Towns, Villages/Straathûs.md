Straathûs is a maritime city of [[the Kaupangsborg]] which sits within the [[Ardalidin Region]] overlooking the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]]. Straathûs is within the borders of the [[Elven Republic]], and maintains independence through economic and military force. Straathûs is a potent naval power and collects tariffs for vessels travelling through the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]].

Straathus has changed hands many times throughout its history and has been an independent city state, part of the [[Bauyokhan Empire]], the [[Elven Republic]], and [[the Kaupangsborg]]. Despite its many theoretical political upheavals, Straathus has in reality been highly stable, with a robust mercantile oligarchy holding the true reigns of power since its founding.
# History
- 283 ES: Straathus was founded in the [[Age of Art]] by the [[Torsten Torstensson|Dread Pirate Torsten]] when he seized several Elven ships blocking the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]] and ran them aground, choosing the bay for its protective fjord and the massive trees that would serve as excellent shipbuilding.
- Torsten seizes a small settled shrine of elven priests that worship the great spirit of the lake.
- The captains and crew of Torsten's armada form a coalition of captains dubbed The Captains of the Coast. The coalition has no codified system of law, relying on the honour of the sea, and a written law develops slowly over time.
- The elven sailors captured by the pirates have their wealth stolen and merge with the native elven population. Many elves stay in the the settlement to protect the spirit of the lake and their claim to the land.
- 241 ES: The elven heralds arrive, and denounce the treatment of the land by the pirates. The natives elves move their settlement to the other side of lake Straathus
- 207 ES: [[Elenwe The Farmer]] convinces [[Graedor The Merchant]] to strike Straathus with a famine. All bounties of the sea pulled from the coast of Straathus becomes poisonous. The labourers of the city riot and strike against the Captains of the Coast. Orlin labourers from the encroaching [[Bauyokhan Empire]] arrive in Straathus to take up the labour of the dead and dying. Priests of Graedor arrive on mission to give comfort and witness during the famine.
- 168 ES: [[Fey Folk]] explorers arrive from [[Hoshi Hall]] on a quest to replenish the ancient trees of Straathus. In return for land, they build into the cliffs of Fjord Edgeholm a series of beautiful mansions for the Captains of the Coast.
- 132 ES: The gentry of Straathus build a wall between north and south Torstentown, further stratifying the class divide. The poorer, North Torstentown begins to swell, while the rich southern portion becomes deeply isolated.
- 118 ES: [[Orlins|Orlin]] mercenaries turn on the Merchant Princes of [[Straathûs]], seizing the city for themselves. Orlins from across the elven realms arrive to Straathus, bringing with them worship of Orthal the Mother.
- 82 ES: Elven priests and Fey druids mix traditions of magic, forming a coven of witches known as the Pagan Not. The coven works to sabotage the exploitation of Straathus' natural resources, in particular, Lake Straathus and the great spirit therein. Orlin population booms as priests of Orthal encourage family values.
- 48 ES: The artistic traditions of the Tankedyr and the Fey Folk mix in North Torsten, creating a new style of art featuring masks. An entertainment guild forms, and builds up North Torsten into a burgeoning entertainment district.
- 6 EF: The new money of South Torsten seek fresh earth to build their family homes. No longer feeling the sense of tradition and dynasty after [[The Memorum Obscura]], they take to the craggy hills of The Rise, creating sprawling mansions at exorbitant cost.
- 42 EF: After 200 years of divine magic, [[Elenwe The Farmer]] turns her gaze towards the ritual of the Pied a Lac, granting them her favour. 8 titanic trees, whose trunk cannot be girdled by less than four score elves and more, immediately sprout in the Stumps. The roots of the trees skewer through the end Fjord Edgeholm, creating a canyon criss crossed with thick wooden roots. The Captains of the Coast seize the land, and begin to profit from the rapid magical growth of the forest, and its strange and divine creatures.
- 89 EF: Offended by Straathus' commercialization of Elenwes miracle, [[Reith Habass Rasul]] seizes [[Straathûs]] in a naval assault
- 144 EF: The Pagan Knot angered by the failure of any rulers to cleanse Lake Straathus, decide to awaken the spirits of the Lake. Angry, malformed spirits of polluted fish and plants roam the streets. The Pagan Knot make pacts with the demons of the deep, cursing the acestors of the Captains of the Coast. The Eleven Republic sends warriors to the city to fight the demons.
- 165 EF: The [[Reith Habass Rasul|Herald of Tides]] asks the sea to make him a bridge to the southern island. Upon its rock he builds a great lighthouse temple named Thunderwatch. Officers of the Elven armada build their homes in Landhalls, next to the original old money families of the Tankedyr.
- 193 EF [[Straathûs]] is purchased from the [[Elven Republic]] by the Merchant Prince [[Jorunn Sokkoldottir]] and joins [[the Kaupangsborg]]
- 201 EF: [[Jorunn Sokkoldottir]] constructs a the Hafkorn, a grand mint, and mints a new currency to solidify the cities joining with [[the Kaupangsborg]]. The coin depicts the Thunderwatch on one side, and the Godstep on the other, honouring the history and people of the city. A new guild, the [[Pearl Keeper's Guild]], forms.

# Landmarks
- Lake Straathus: A dingy lagoon where sleeps a great spirit
- The Rise: a series of hills to the west, ending in sharp cliffs that overlook [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]]
- Fjord Edgeholm: a long narrow fjord, where many old families of straathus build their homes along the walls
- The Godstep: a series of small lakes in the shape of a pawprint, where once stepped[[Graedor The Merchant]] in days of old
- The Dawnhall and the Bilge Beach: The Damnation, the original ship of [[Torsten Torstensson]], now serves as the cornerstone of the Dawnhall (faces east), a hall where the merchant princes/guidmasters who run the city meet to govern
- The Tree Dome: A tori gate made of pine trees grown in a twisting, covered tunnel of twisting trees. The original shrine of the native elven popualtion
- Temple of Orthal
- Library of the Pagan knot
