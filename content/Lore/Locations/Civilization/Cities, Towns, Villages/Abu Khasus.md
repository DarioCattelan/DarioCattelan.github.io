# Overview

Abu Khasus is a city in the [[Elven Republic]] on the Southern coast of the [[Kaiko-chiri Strait]]. It is a relatively new city, having seen a huge boom in population following an influx of refugees during [[Mothers' Heresy]].
# History
Abu Khasus saw a boom in population following [[Mothers' Heresy]], when refugees fled the lands that would become [[Netham]] to settle in the region. The [[Reith Habass Rasul|Herald of Tides]] was instrumental in the survival of the refugees who would emigrate to Abu Khasus, and is considered the patron Herald of the city.
