# Overview
Amissa, or the Lost Continent, is a landmass surrounded by islands in the Southeast of the [[Corporeal World]]. It is the only other continent in the Corporeal World aside from [[Kaloshen]]. Several legends speak of fearsome and savage curses that scour the land. Travel to Amissa is forbidden by decree of [[The Fundamentals]].

# History
In the [[Journey of the Steppe]] [[Orthal The Mother]] asks the [[Herald of Unity]] to connect the Lost Continent (believed to be Amissa) to [[Kaloshen]], but they ultimately end their quest before reaching the surrounding islands.
