The Corporeal World is the physical presence of [[Kaloshen]] where the bodies of mortals exist. It is the material plane of creation, and intersects [[the Warrens]]. Most believe that the Corporeal World was created by [[The Fundamentals]], though the [[Tallmen]] of [[Friðstow]] believe the Corporeal World is the manifestation of [[Gast]].
