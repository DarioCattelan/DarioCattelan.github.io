The Warrens are the extra-planar byways and passages that exist behind the [[Corporeal World]]. Long distances in the [[Corporeal World]] can often be crossed by shorter corridors in the Warrens. It is said that the Warrens bridge the [[Corporeal World]] past [[The Godwall]] to [[The Afterlife]].

[[The Nomad]] has domain over the Warrens ever since [[The Victoria Viae]], and was trapped in the Warrens by [[Elenwe The Farmer]] and [[Dwemeran The Collector]] during the events of [[The Wæl]]. Although no one has seen the Nomad in many years, the [[Heralds]] have said that it is his theft of travellers' [[Breath]] as they cross the Warrens to the Afterlife that is spawning [[Undead]] in the [[Corporeal World]].
