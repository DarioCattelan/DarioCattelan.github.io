# Overview
The Kaiko-chiri Strait is a long, narrow straight in the North of [[Kaloshen]] that bisects the [[Elven Republic]] and leads into the mainland of the North to [[Netham]]. It is a critical maritime trade route between the west coast and the North.

The mouth of the pass is controlled by [[the Kaupangsborg]] city of [[Straathûs]], and is highly contested territory.
