# Overview
The Khadan mountains are a series of mountains in the north east of [[Kaloshen]] directly north of [[Noitanga]]. It borders the ancestral lands of several [[Orlins|Orlin]] tribes, including the Tsagaani, Zagashi, and Zogii.
