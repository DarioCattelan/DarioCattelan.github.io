
The Sonokar Stone is an ancient obelisk located inside [[the Warrens]]. The stone is carved with many [[Nethanese]] runes, the meaning of which has been lost to time following [[The Wæl]].

It is believed that the stone is the cage of [[Sonokar Chiri]], a Great Spirit from the [[Age of Spirits]]. The stone has been guarded by the [[Coven of the Hateful Hare]], supposedly since its founding in the [[Age of Spirits]].
