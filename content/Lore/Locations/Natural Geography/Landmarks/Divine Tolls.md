Divine Tolls are large crater imbued with a powerful [[Arcana]], each Toll manifesting different effects. When they have occasion to manifest, the [[Spirits#Chiri|Chiri]] of the Tolls are powerfully magical and the subject of local worship. There are 6 Tolls, one for each of the gods.

# Origin of the Tolls
There is no agreed upon origin of the Tolls. The two major prevailing theories are the Divine Barrow Theory, and the Origin of Creation Theory.

## Origin of Creation Theory
According [[Elves|Elven]], [[Orlins|Orlin]], and [[Fey Folk|Fey]] mythology, the Tolls are where [[The Fundamentals]] first began the creation of [[Kaloshen]].

## Divine Barrow Theory
According to [[Dwarves|Dwarf]], [[Tankedyr]] and [[Halflings|Halfling]] mythology, the Tolls are the barrows of the [[Demigods]] buried during the [[Mors Liberorum]].
