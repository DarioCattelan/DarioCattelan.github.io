# Overview
The Uul Mountains are a series of snow-capped mountains in the northwest of [[Kaloshen]], separating the [[Orlin Plains]] and the [[Almam Steppe]]. The foothills of the mountains are the ancestral lands of the Tsagaani [[The Olon|Olon tribe]] and the Tarvagi [[The Suutin|Suutin tribe]].
