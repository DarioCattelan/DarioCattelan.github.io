
Divine Tolls are large craters where, according to legend, [[the Fundamentals]] first began the creation of [[Kaloshen]]. The Tolls are imbued with a powerful [[Arcana]], and each Toll's arcana manifests different effects. When they have occasion to manifest, the [[Spirits#Chiri|Chiri]] of the Tolls are powerfully magical and the subject of local worship. There is one Toll for each of the gods.
