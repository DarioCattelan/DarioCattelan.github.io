The Memorum Obscura is a curse placed upon [[axiomatic mortals]] during the events of [[The Wæl]]. The Memorum Obscura magically muddles the memories of [[Perpetuals]], causing their recollections from before the Wæl to be inconsistent and unreliable. It is believed that the Memorum Obscura is the work of [[The Nomad]].
