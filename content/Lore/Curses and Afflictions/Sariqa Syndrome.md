Sariqa Syndrome, sometimes called the Slow Obscura after [[The Wæl#Memorum Obscura|Memorum Obscura]], is a degenerative mental curse which slowly affects the ability to recall memories. It has a slow rate of progression, often taking centuries to fully incapacitate the afflicted, making it a minor curse to all [[axiomatic mortals]] save for [[Elves]]. Longtime sufferers can eventually degenerate to a childlike state.
