Arcana is magic that is tied to a place or object. Unlike [[Breath]], Arcana does not need to originate from a living creature, and can arise naturally. Arcana includes [[Breath]] that is bound to an object or place, temporarily or otherwise.

# History
Before the discovery of [[Runes of Power]] by [[Oberron the Carver]], Arcana could only be permanently imbued into objects by [[The Fundamentals]], or develop Arcana over the course of several ages.
