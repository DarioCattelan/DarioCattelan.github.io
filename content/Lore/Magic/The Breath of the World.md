

**Pathfinder equivalent:** Primal spellcasting

The Breath of the World is a [[Breath#Methods of Breath|method of Breath]] characterized by carefully controlling and bolstering one's own [[Breath]] through bodily mastery. Unlike [[The Breath of Tales]], the method cannot be learned, and additionally unlike [[The Breath of Devotion]] it cannot be gifted, a mage has to be born with the natural talent. The Breath of the World is a dominant hereditary trait, and the [[Primeval bloodlines]] are families with the talent for the Breath of the World.

The subject of which bloodline first developed the Breath of the World is a topic of much debate.
