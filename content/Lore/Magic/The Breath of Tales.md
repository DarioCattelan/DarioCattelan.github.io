

**Pathfinder 2e equivalent:** Occult spellcasting

The Breath of Tales is a [[Breath#Temperament|Cold, Humid]] [[Breath#Methods of Breath|method of Breath]] that focuses [[Breath]] by leveraging, but not necessarily understanding, breath sparks.

[[Humans]] are supposedly the originator of the Breath of Tales, but this is rarely acknowledge and highly debated by mages of the [[axiomatic mortals]].

# Breath sparks
Breath sparks are a kind of [[Breath#Breath Ignitions|Breath ignition]] that takes the form of songs, stories, dances, names, and other miscellaneous and sometimes unpredictable actions that bolster the caster's [[Breath]] with power from ambient and unknown origins. Unlike [[The Breath of the World]], the Breath of Tales is not something that is innate to ones [[Breath]], but learned by collecting breath sparks. Conversely, mages as of yet have been unable to determine the mechanism by which a breath spark bolsters Breath, and therefore unlike the mythological [[The Breath of the Mind|Breath of the Mind]], a mage who knows several breath sparks cannot use them to derive or create new breath sparks.
