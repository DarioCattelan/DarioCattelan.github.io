

**Pathfinder equivalent:** Arcane spellcasting

The Breath of the Mind is a mythological [[Breath#Methods of Breath|method of Breath]] that may or may not have existed. Folklorists argue that if the Breath of the Mind existed, it would not have even been a true [[Breath#Methods of Breath|method of Breath]], but rather a system by which one could *create* methods of [[Breath]].

Legend holds that through long and careful study of [[Breath]], anyone could develop a general model for creating personal [[The Breath of Tales#Breath sparks|Breath sparks]] attuned to their own [[Breath#Colours & hue|Hue]]. In the fables, the Breath of the Mind is completely outside the purview of [[The Fundamentals]], having been created by mortals after the gods had finished their work.
