The Breath of the Mind is a mythological [[Breath#Methods of Breath|method of Breath]] that may or may not have existed. Folklorists argue that if the Breath of the Mind existed, it would not have even been a true [[Breath#Methods of Breath|method of Breath]], but rather a system by which one could *create* methods of [[Breath]].

Legend holds that through long and careful study of [[Breath]], the wicked [[Tumultuarii]] could create  [[Breath#Breath Ignitions|Breath ignitions]] attuned to their own [[Breath#Colours & hue|Hue]]. In the fables, the Breath of the Mind is completely outside the purview of [[The Fundamentals]], having been created by mortals after the gods had finished their work. As such, it is blaspheme to attempt the Breath of the Mind.
