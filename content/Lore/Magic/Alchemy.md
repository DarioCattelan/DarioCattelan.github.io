Alchemy is the art of potions, salves, elixirs, powders, vapors, and a host of other reagents. It is a distinct form of magic unrelated to [[Arcana]] or [[Breath]], and does not always align with the [[Breath#The Eight Colours|Colours]] of Breath.

# Alchemical Schools
Alchemy is divided into two schools; Ancient Alchemy, and Chemical Alchemy (sometimes called Mundane Alchemy).
## Ancient Alchemy
Ancient alchemy refers to consumable alchemical solutions that use a honey base, typical potions or elixirs, and who's chemical reaction produces an effect to [[Breath]] or [[Arcana]]. It is so dubbed "Ancient" as it was the first school of alchemy discovered during the [[Age of Spirits]] by the witch [[Orisin]] and their familiar [[Jobachi]].

Ancient Alchemy is difficult to produce reliably, and is highly influenced by the Breath of the alchemist or the Arcana of its ingredients and brewing location. As such, the arts of Ancient Alchemy are often passed between sedentary guilds whose members have a similar [[Breath#Colours & hue|Hue]], such as [[Witches Coven|Witches Covens]].

Although one might assume from its classification that Ancient Alchemy does not entail any chemical reactions, this is in fact a misnomer and a common misconception.

## Chemical Alchemy
Chemical Alchemy, sometimes called Mundane Alchemy, refers to alchemical solutions, powders, or vapors that do not produce an effect to [[Breath]] or [[Arcana]]. These kinds of reagents are categorically without [[Breath#The Eight Colours|Magical Colour]] and have no magical aura.

Chemical Alchemy is universally reproduceable, and has its origins in [[Tanji's Innovative Method]] (TIM). Any alchemical process that is properly documented in the TIM can be reproduced to exact specification based solely on the mundane skill of the alchemist.

Chemical Alchemy was invented by [[Kgali Inara]] during the [[Age of Art]].
