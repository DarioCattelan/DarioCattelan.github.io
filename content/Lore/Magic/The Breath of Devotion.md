
**Pathfinder equivalent:** Divine spellcasting

The Breath of Devotion is a [[Breath#Temperament|Hot, Humid]] [[Breath#Methods of Breath|method of Breath]] by which a sapient entity bolsters the [[Breath]] of another creature. Like [[The Breath of Tales]], and the mythical [[The Breath of the Mind|Breath of the Mind]], a mage does not need to be born with a natural predisposition for the Breath of Devotion. Instead, the Breath of Devotion is a talent gifted to a mage from a patron.

# Exclamations
An exclamation is a kind of [[Breath#Breath Ignitions|Breath ignition]] where the mage makes a request to an external patron to bolster their Breath. The exclamation is not necessarily verbal, and can sometimes be communicated telepathically or through the use of a material component. Patrons who have empowered a follower in the past are sometimes unable to resist an exclamation, and there are several recorded instances where [[The Fundamentals]] themselves have not been aware that they had responded to an exclamation.
