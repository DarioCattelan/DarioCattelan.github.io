
The Deeplings are mythical creatures from the fable the [[Trial of Maolan]]. They are a race of cunning cave dwelling creatures who try to consume [[Maolan Antraoir|Maolan]] and the [[Ice People]]. In the fable, [[Maolan Antraoir|Maolan]] recalls an ancient bit of wisdom about the Deeplings, and uses it to steal their [[Breath]].
