The Deeplings are mythical creatures from the fable the [[Trial of Maji]]. They are a race of cunning cave dwelling creatures who try to consume [[Maji of the Mountain|Maji]] and the [[Ice People]]. In the fable, Maji recalls an ancient bit of wisdom about the Deeplings, and uses it to steal their [[Breath]].
