

Devils are a classification of pact-seeking [[Spirits]]. The exact taxonomy of [[Spirits]] is somewhat arbitrary, but a spirit is generally considered a Devil if it meets the following criteria:

1. It is pact-seeking
2. It can manifest at will
3. It can be summoned

Most cultures shun Devils and their pacts, with the exception of [[Tankedyr]] in the city states of [[the Kaupangsborg]].
