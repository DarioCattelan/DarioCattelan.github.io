The Demigods were children of [[axiomatic mortals]] and [[The Fundamentals]]. They were mighty mortal [[Perpetuals]] with powerful [[Breath]]. Depictions of the demigods vary greatly, with some having a relatively humanoid shape, while other's are more monstrous in nature.

The Fundamentals forbade each other from siring Demigods during the [[Age of Gentle Waters]], and slew the remaining Demigods in an event known as the [[Mors Liberorum]]. It is not known why the Gods made this decision.
