# Overview
Tallmen are [[axiomatic mortals]] created by the traitorous [[The Fundamentals|Fundamental]] [[The Nomad]].[^1] Tallmen are an endangered species that live in a secluded community far away in the mountains of [[Kaloshen]]. The existence of Tallmen is not known by other [[axiomatic mortals]] and is possibly hidden from [[The Fundamentals]] themselves.

Tallmen despise [[The Fundamentals]], blaming them for [[The Wæl]], a calamity that nearly wiped out their race.

Tallmen are so named for their size in comparison to the other [[Humans|Human]] race of [[Halflings]], who are several feet shorter in stature.

# Identity

## Homeland
Tallmen live in [[Friðstow]], a walled mountain village more fortress than township. With a population numbering in low hundreds, the Tallmen of [[Friðstow]] are a tightly knit and self-reliant community of survivors. Life is difficult in the cold arboreal mountains, and it is extremely rare for a Tallmen to have the opportunity to leave [[Friðstow]]. Most Tallmen have three lifepaths they can take in [[Friðstow]]: 
-  a tough but proud agrarian life supporting the community
- to renounce community and study the wards and ancestral lore with the cloistered Galdor
-  or for the brave and foolish, to survive the training beyond the walls to become a Wiga, a warrior monk of [[Friðstow]]
