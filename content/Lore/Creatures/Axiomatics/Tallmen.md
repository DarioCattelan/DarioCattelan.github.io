# Overview
> [!NOTE] Pathfinder Possible Ancestries
> Human

Tallmen are [[axiomatic mortals]] created by the traitorous [[The Fundamentals|Fundamental]] [[The Nomad]].[^1] Tallmen are an endangered species that live in a secluded community far away in the mountains of [[Kaloshen]]. The existence of Tallmen is not known by other [[axiomatic mortals]] and is possibly hidden from [[The Fundamentals]] themselves.

Tallmen despise [[The Fundamentals]], blaming them for [[The Wæl]], a calamity that nearly wiped out their race.

Tallmen are so named for their size in comparison to the other [[Humans|Human]] race of [[Halflings]], who are several feet shorter in stature.

# Identity

## Homeland
Tallmen live in [[Friðstow]], a walled mountain village more fortress than township. With a population numbering in low hundreds, the Tallmen of [[Friðstow]] are a tightly knit and self-reliant community of survivors. Life is difficult in the cold arboreal mountains, and it is extremely rare for a Tallman to have the opportunity to leave [[Friðstow]]. Most Tallmen have three lifepaths they can take in [[Friðstow]]: 
-  a tough but proud agrarian life supporting the community
- a cloistered, ascetic life studying wards and ancestral lore as a Galdor
-  or for the brave and foolish, a harsh, martial life of training beyond the walls to become a Wiga, a warrior monk of [[Friðstow]]

### The Soþgast
The Soþgast (SOOTH-gahst), or simply [[Soþgast]], is the system of believe of the Tallmen of [[Friðstow]]. Unlike followers of the [[axiomatist]] religions, Tallmen do not believe that [[Kaloshen]] was created by [[The Fundamentals]]. Instead, believers of [[Soþgast]] believe that [[Kaloshen]] itself is a great [[Spirits|Spirit]] called Gast, and that all corporeal life are aspects of Gast who have forgotten their origins.

This belief system is highly controversial to followers of [[axiomatist]] religions, as it questions [[The Fundamentals]] right to rule, and establishes [[axiomatic mortals]] as the same order of being as the gods.

Tallmen are not a particularly orthodox people, and not all Tallmen are believers in the dogma of [[Soþgast]]. That being said, the faith is strongly embedded in the culture of [[Friðstow]], and as a result, even the faithless bear a deep respect for [[Spirits]] and a distain for [[The Fundamentals]].

### Galdor
Galdor (GAL-door) are an ascetic [[Soþgast]] order of monk scholars. Like Wiga, the presence of Galdor at [[Friðstow]] predates [[The Wæl]]. The Galdor are an acetic, cloistered order, who prior to [[The Wæl]] did not have contact with the rest of [[Kaloshen]], and did not allow women into their ranks. Though they still maintain their ascetic values, due to the necessity of the times the Galdor are relatively integrated with the community of [[Friðstow]], and all Tallmen can join their ranks.

The Galdor are the keepers of religion and lore in [[Friðstow]], and maintain the historical cultural practices of the Tallmen in their library. Unfortunately, the vast majority of Galdor texts are written in [[Nethanese]], and are no longer legible without a lifetime of research after the events of [[the Wæl]]. One of the main duties of the Galdor order has been to research and translate their library into Common, and each monk will have a tome assigned to them for life. With so few [[Tallmen]], and even fewer who become Galdors, the progress is slow even after 200 years of study.

The Galdor are led by a Head Abbot.

#### Gemyndan Mass
Galdor are not only researchers, but also serve as cultural leaders and teachers in [[Friðstow]]. Galdor Brothers (which is no longer a gender exclusive rank) hold a weekly ritual called Gemyndan (ge-MEWN-dan) Mass, where they read from translated texts and teach what is known of the history of [[Tallmen]] . Gemyndan Mass is where children learn to read and write [[Common]], a practice that has become sacred to all [[Tallmen]].

### Wiga
Wiga (wee-GHA) are a martial order of [[Soþgast]] monks who's presence at [[Friðstow]] predates [[the Wæl]]. Wiga are guardians and hunters who range the frigid mountains, ensuring the last fortress of the [[Tallmen]] are safe from the fearsome beasts and malevolent spirits that roam the land. Wiga spend their lives learning methods of de-manifesting enraged [[Spirits]] in a way that calms their [[Breath]] and does no permanent harm. As a [[Soþgast]] order, many Wiga would rather be killed that injure or kill a Spirit that could contain a universe unto itself. The Wiga are lead by a Grand Master Wiga.

#### Wiga pilgrimage
Wiga have an arduous training regime which culminates in a pilgrimage to 8 remote shrines in the mountains. The way to each shrine is not maintained, and it is the duty of the Wiga aspirant to overcome any obstacles they encounter on their own. Upon reaching each of the 8 shrines, an aspirant must clean and repair the shrine, and make an offering of food that they have carried untouched through their pilgrimage. At the final shrine, the Wiga is allowed to partake of the offering, which is traditionally made of a holy mushroom cultivated by the Galdor. It is said that at the last shrine a Wiga often experiences a life changing vision which helps direct their purpose in the order. The exact nature of the vision is highly personal, and shared only with the head of the Wiga order. 

Unlike the Galdor, being a Wiga is not a lifelong commitment, and many a brash youth has attempted the lifestyle of a Wiga only to return home. 

### Witches and Warlocks
Some Galdor of Wiga monks who have a particularly powerful attunement to [[Spirits]] can sometimes form a connection with a great Spirit, and can forgo research into [[Nethanese]] and martial training in favor of their harmony as Gast. In such instances, the [[Breath]] of a monk can grow deeply from connecting with their Spirit patron, and they can gain [[The Breath of Devotion]]. Such monks have special duties in [[Friðstow]] to ensure the harmony between [[Tallmen]] and [[Spirits]], and are called Witches or Warlocks. With its tiny population, [[Friðstow]] is lucky to have even a single Witch or Warlock at any given time.

## Language
Tallmen's native language has become [[Common]], and all Tallmen learn to read and write the language from a young age.

## Organization
Tallmen are led by a patrilineal Chiefdom with power derived by the fealty of the [[Soþgast]] orders, and is advised by a tribal council. The positions of the head of the Galdor and Wiga are appointed by their respective orders, and the tribe's witch or warlock has only ever had a single eligible occupant, but otherwise all the positions in the council are appointed by the Chief themselves and serve at pleasure.

### Armed forces
The Wiga form the only formal armed forces of [[Friðstow]], though occasionally a civilian militia will be created to fend off a particularly ravenous group of mountain beasts or similar. The Wiga are not beholden to follow the orders of the Chief, but are instead commanded by the Grand Master. The Grand Master, however, swears an oath of fealty to the Chief and his line, and their power is often held in check by the court Witch or Warlock, who's abilities are an unknown.

### Public Services
Almost all public services, including administration, health, and education are handled by the Galdor. Constabulary services are an exception and are run by a Sheriff appointed by the Chief.


### Heralds
The Tallmen have no [[Heralds]], the Tallmen want no Heralds, the Tallmen need no Heralds.

# Biology
Tallmen are biologically equivalent to real world Human.

# History
The [[Tallmen]] do not know much of their history. Below is what has been passed down from survivors or translated from ancient lore.

- The Tallmen were once a prosperous people in the land of [[Old Netham]] (simply Netham to Tallmen, who are not aware of its refounding)
- [[The Fundamentals|The Fundamental]] who cared for the [[Tallmen]], [[The Nomad]], betrayed the other gods[^2] and their ire fell upon [[Old Netham]]
- The Tallmen were all but eradicated in the following calamity dubbed [[the Wæl]]
- A few dozen survivors managed to reach [[Friðstow]] and start a new life


# Relationships

The other [[axiomatic mortals]] do not know of the existence of Tallmen in anything other than myth and legend.
