
The Silvaen Tapestry is a magical tapestry woven during the [[Age of Witches]] which depicts the events of [[The Pactum Silvae]] when [[Hoshi Hall]] was hidden in [[the Warrens]]. It is the oldest known tapestry in existence, and was originally commissioned by [[Titania The Muse]] to hang in her throne room. During the [[Age of the Pillar]] the tapestry was stolen by [[The Gadian Ri]], and is now in the possession of [[Dwemeran The Collector]].

A copy of the tapestry was gifted to Titania by Dwemeran after the events of [[The Wæl]], and hangs in the [[Royal Conservatory of the Arts]] in Hoshi Hall.
