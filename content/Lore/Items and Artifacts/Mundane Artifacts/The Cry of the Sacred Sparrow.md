*The Cry of the Sacred Sparrow* is an epic pseudo-historical poem by [[Lady Belladonna]]. It is a dramatized telling of the events surrounding [[Mothers' Heresy]], including the Orlin civil war, and the refounding of [[Netham]]. It is considered one of the greatest artistic works of the [[Age of the Five]].
