
The Music Box of Delights is an artifact created by [[The Nomad]] during the [[Age of the Elders]] which could sing songs that foretold of happy moments to come in the future. The box was ultimately a deceit given to the elves of the [[Ageless Empire]], as it did not forewarn them of [[The Shifting]], which precipitated the fall of their empire.
