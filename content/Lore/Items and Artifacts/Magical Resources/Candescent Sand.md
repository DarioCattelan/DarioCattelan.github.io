Candescent Sand is fine, powdery sand that changes colour when exposed to [[Breath]] or [[Arcana]]. This property makes candescent sand a hugely valuable resource, and its first discovery in the foothills of the [[Budsan Desert]] by the [[Halflings]] of [[Old Netham]] precipitated the [[Age of Art]].

Candescent sand can be ground as a fine pigment of virtually any colour, formed into [[Arcana]] retaining glass and pottery, and serves as a reagent in [[Alchemy]].
