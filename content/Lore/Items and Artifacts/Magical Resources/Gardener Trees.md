The Gardener trees are eight titanic trees miraculously grown in [[Straathûs]] by [[Elenwe The Farmer]] to recognize the devotion of the [[Coven of the Guilty Gardener]]. The trees provide [[Straathûs]] and endless supply of wood, and attracts many strange creatures and spirits.

The trees have a devastating consequence for the city. Requiring such an intense life-draining arcana to persist, the trees leave the landscape surrounding [[Straathûs]] barren and devoid of all but the most resilient plant life.
