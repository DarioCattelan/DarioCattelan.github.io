
The Septors Seven are the 7 mythological scepters of the Elvin High Kings of the [[Ageless Empire]]. The Septors Seven were said to each have a deep and powerful [[Arcana]], and were some of the greatest magical wonders of the [[Age of the Elders]]. The Septors were said to have been lost during [[The Shifting]]. Near the end of the [[Age of the Pillar]], [[Marrowgorn]] bore a scepter that they claimed was the [[Fortnight Septor]], one of the Septors Seven.
