# Overview

The Cataclysm Blade, sometimes called the Blade of Disaster, is an ancient flint bastard sword crafted by the [[Ice People]] during the [[Age of the Elders]] said to hold terrible power. It was thought lost during the [[Age of Gentle Waters]], but eventually made its way into the hands of the [[Prodiji Shah]] as the symbol of the [[Hevlon Ibara Rasul|Herald of Disaster]].
# Appearance
A bastard sword made of ocher coloured flint, covered in a thick resin, and bearing a hilt and pommel of polished ivory. It is accompanied by an ivory sheath of tusk and bone bound my gut-string and leather, with a heavy arcana preserving it from decay over millennia.
# Abilities
It is said that the drawing of the Cataclysm Blade from its scabbard heralds a terrible disaster. Despite its brittle appearance, the flint of its body never chips or shatters, and tales tell of the blade cleaving through metal and stone.
