The Unfailing Rod is a mythical artifact of [[Dwemeran The Collector]] that he gave to [[Elenwe The Farmer]] during the [[Edict of Anestrus]]. The rod is counted as one of the [[Septors Seven]].

It was a fishing rod which never failed to hook its target, with an arm so long that it could pass over [[The Godwall]], and a hook so sharp it could pierce a soul. In [[Edict of Anestrus#The Fable|the Fable]], [[Elenwe The Farmer]] uses the rod to pull back the souls of dead elves, ensuring that they will reincarnate.
