
The Milk of the Moon was a silvery magical potion crafted by [[Titania The Muse]] during the [[Age of the Elders]] and offered to [[Elenwe The Farmer]] to help enact the [[Edict of Anestrus]]. The milk was said to be harvested from the [[Spirits|Spirit]] of the Moon, and could turn an [[axiomatic mortals|axiomatic mortal]] into a different race. The fable of the [[Edict of Anestrus]] does not specify if the Milk of the Moon was ever used.
