# Overview
Cornucopia is an arcane cooking pot and symbolic artifact of the [[Hasada Qesan Rasul|Herald of the Harvest]]. It was originally crafted by [[Ivar Ivarson]] during the [[Age of Orlin]].
# Appearance
A small copper saucepan with a dark wooden handle, worn smooth and shiny from the grasp of a hundred hundred hands.
# Abilities
The exact abilities of Cornucopia is not well known, however, a few properties have been recorded in esoteric texts and records
- The pot can hold far more broth than its exterior volume would allow
- The pot can serve more food than the sum of ingredients used
- Food served from the pot grants a calming, comforting effect on those who eat it
