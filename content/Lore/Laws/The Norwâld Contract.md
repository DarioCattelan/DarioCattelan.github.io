The Nordwald Contract is an legal agreement between the [[Elven Republic]] and the [[the Kaupangsborg]] city state of [[Straathûs]] on the taxation and administration of the forest region of [[Norwâld]]. The contract is the establishing charter of the [[Norwâld Territory Administration]].

# Official Name
This contract is legally known as "A contract between the Elven Republic and Kaupangsborg of Straathûs regarding the administration of strategic territory and trade facilitation for the benefit of sovereign nations in relative entente". It is informally referred to as "The Norwâld Contract" or "The Norwâld Charter" depending on if the speaker is [[Tankedyr]] or the [[Elves|Elven]] respectively.

# Duration
The Norwald Contract is valid in perpetuity, or until the decommissioning of the [[Norwâld Territory Administration]].

# Controversy
The Norwald Contract is a source of controversy to some signatories of [[The Freewalk Accords]], as it allows the lawful taxation of travelers through [[Norwâld]]. Opposition to the charter argue that such taxation is a legally imposed barrier to emigration, which is a violation of the accords.
