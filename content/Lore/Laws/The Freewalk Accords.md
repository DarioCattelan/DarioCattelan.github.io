
The Freewalk Accords is a peace treatise that marked the end of [[Mothers' Heresy]]. The accord was made between the [[Orlins|Orlin]] and the other [[axiomatic mortals]] , and declared that no axiomatic race would have legal hegemony over another.

As part of the peace deal, the accord returned of most of the ancestral lands of [[Netham]] to the diasporic [[Halflings]].

The Freewalk Accords also enshrined the right of emigration in its signatories, granting the right of any mortal to emigrate from a signatory nation unopposed.

Of all the nations of [[Kaloshen]], only a small number of [[the Kaupangsborg]] refused to sign the treatise.
