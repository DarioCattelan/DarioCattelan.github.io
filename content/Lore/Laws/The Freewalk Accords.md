The Freewalk Accords is a peace treatise that marked the end of [[Mothers' Heresy]]. The accord was made between the [[Orlins|Orlin]] and the other [[axiomatic mortals]] , and declared that no axiomatic race would have legal hegemony over another.

# Return of Netham
As part of the peace deal, the accord returned of most of the ancestral lands of [[Netham]] to the diasporic [[Halflings]].
# Right of emigration
The Freewalk Accords also enshrined the right of emigration in its signatories, granting the right of any mortal to emigrate from a signatory nation unopposed. This has had a profound effect on the economic systems of the [[Elven Republic]] and the [[Dwarven Fiefs]], effectively ending legal serfdom.
# Exile of Grastus
As part of the accords, the hero [[Grastus the Free]] who led [[Mothers' Heresy]] was exiled to an island on the [[Divine Tolls|Mazarie Toll]] and forbidden to participate in the politics of the world.
# Non-signatories
Of all the nations of [[Kaloshen]], only a small number of [[the Kaupangsborg]] refused to sign the treatise.
