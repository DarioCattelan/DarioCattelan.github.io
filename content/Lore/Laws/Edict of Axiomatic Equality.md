# Overview
The Edict of Axiomatic Equality was an epiphany granted to [[Grastus the Free]] from [[Orthal The Mother]] which enlightened the Herald on the true nature of the [[The Ordning]] and appointed him as Herald of Orthal. Prior to this epiphany, the [[Herald of Equality|Herald of Royalty]] had by tradition been the ruler of the [[Ahltsuki]] Khanate, and the edict greatly disrupted the status quo of the empire.
From this epiphany, the [[Orlins|Orlin]] came to believe that all [[The Ordning#Ordning Links|Ordning Links]] were without division, and all of creation was equal within its own Link. After the edict was announced, the Herald of Royalty was reconsecrated the [[Herald of Equality]].

# Outcomes and Impacts
Unwilling to relinquish the Ordning based caste system and the station of Herald that kept them in power, the ruling [[Ahltsuki]] tribe refused to recognize the epiphany, opposing the supposed will of [[Orthal The Mother]]. In opposition, [[Grastus the Free]] raised the [[Three People's Army]], composed of Orlin from all castes, sparking rebellion against the Ahltsuki Khanate known as [[The Orlin Civil War]]. Grastus' forces were ultimately victorious, dissolving the Orlin caste system.

Perhaps emboldened by their success, the [[Three People's Army]] declared the [[Dwarves]], [[Elves]], and [[Tankedyr]] also in violation of the Edict, invading their nearby lands in what would come to be known as [[Mothers' Heresy]].
