

The Fundamentals are the corporeal Gods of Kaloshen. Each Fundamental created one of the [[axiomatic mortals|axiomatic mortal races]], and rules over them directly. Each Fundamental also has one or more [[Heralds|Herald]], an axiomatic mortal who represents their desires.
