The Codex is the encyclopedia of processes, procedures, traditions, ideologies, grudges, and theories developed by [[Tanji's Innovative Method]]. It is considered the holy scripture of [[Esoteric Timism]].
# Origin
It is believed that the first copy of the Codex was given to [[The Gadian Ri]] during the [[Age of Witches]] in 682 [[Era of the Five|ES]]. This early version of the Codex had but a few simple traditions concerning [[Dwarves#Succession|Dwarven Succession]] which are still observed today. Upon receiving the Codex, legend holds that [[Dwemeran The Collector]] bade the Gadian Ri to find the worlds greatest scholar to be the Codex's steward. In the tale, the Gadian Ri spends all season preparing for a great pilgrimage across the land, only to find and deliver the Codex to [[Tanji Inquondo]] less than a week later.

# Interpretation in Dwarven Law
The Codex is typically used as the source for legal decision making in the [[Dwarven Fiefs]] as interpreted by [[Esoteric Timism#Clerics of TIM|Clerics of TIM]].
