Truhuggun (TROO-hoo-gun) is the traditional faith of the [[Tankedyr]]. It is a monotheistic [[axiomatist]] religion that preaches the maximizing of comfort and rewards worshipers with a permanent, idyllic afterlife.

**Symbols:** The Sunning Snake

# Core Beliefs
1. There is an afterlife, where an [[axiomatic mortals|axiomatic mortal's]] soul lives on infinitely after death
2. The comfort one has created in life is evaluated and brought with them into the afterlife
3. There is one true God who judges the value of a soul: [[Graedor The Merchant]]

## The Halls of Silk
The Halls of Silk is the name of the heavenly afterlife of Truhuggun. It is a place of luxury and pleasure, where all the comfort that were the result of one's actions in life are delivered back to the faithful for eternity.

# The Chantry of Truhuggun
The Chantry of Truhuggun is the theocratic governing body of Truhuggun. It is a hierarchical priesthood, with [[Graedor The Merchant]] serving as its head. 

# Rituals

## Asmenn
Asmenn (ahz-MEN) is the weekly gathering of the faithful of Truhuggun in a place of worship to listen to a sermon delivered by a priest of the faith. The ceremony usually begins with worshippers placing the food they have brought for the communal meal up to the priesthood while a liturgical song or poem is recited. There is then a call for monetary donations to the Chantry. Finally, the Asmenn is closed with a shared meal from dishes brought by the congregants.
