The Nihir Lawall (nee-HEER la-WHALL), or "the Way of the Cycle" in [[Common]], is the codified religion of the [[Elves]] from [[Elenwe The Farmer]]. It is an [[axiomatist]] animist religion that teaches that the [[Corporeal World]] has a cyclical rhythm to its being that is empowered by regular observation of harmonious rituals. Followers of the religion believe that the souls of [[Elves]] are perpetual, and will reincarnate from beyond [[The Godwall]], and that [[Spirits]] were once elves who had achieved perfect harmony during their life.

# Core Beliefs
1. Repetition brings us closer to divine perfection
2. There exist key moments in the stream of time with great power
3. With age comes wisdom, with wisdom comes divinity
4. The soul is eternal, and will reincarnate in harmony with the last life lived

# The Church of Mazarie
The Church of Mazarie (ma-ZA-ree), or "Church of the Farmer" in [[Common]], is the centralized governing theocratic institution of the [[Nihir Lawall]].

The churches role is to interpret patterns and auguries of the [[Corporeal World]] and the will of [[Elenwe The Farmer|Elenwe]] in order to determine which [[Elves#Traditions & Beliefs|mutacurir and alnucur]] should be observed at a national level. Additionally, the Church of Mazarie serves as the intake body for [[Elves#cilaf|cilaf]] which have reached a level of esoterism such that it requires broad dissemination amongst the elder elves. 

## The Mazaram

The Mazaram (ma-ZA-ram) are the priests of the [[Nihir Lawall#The Church of Mazarie|The Church of Mazarie]]. Unlike the [[Elves#The Majilis|Majilis]], the Mazaram is not a gerontocracy and its membership is open to any elf. There is no strict hegemony in the Church of Mazarie, but administration of the Church is a rotating membership of 8 positions called the Shah Mazaram (often called the Shahram to distinguish from the [[Elves#The Shah|Prodiji Shah]]). Every 128 years, 64 Mazaram are chosen to serve two yearly terms each during that period as Shah Mazaram.

## The Shah Mazaram and the Majilis

The [[Elves#The Majilis|Elven Majilis]] is required to yield time to members of the Shah Mazaram during a special session each season to hear the season's required rituals. None but [[Elenwe The Farmer|the High Tari]] is able to dispute the ritual needs of the Shahram for the season, but are required to decide where the resources for the rituals will come from. The Majilis cannot break their session or pass to new legislation until the Shahram yield the floor.

# Beliefs

## Reincarnation
The faithful of Nihir Lawall hold that [[Elenwe The Farmer]], goddess of cycles, judges the souls of dead [[Elves]] as they pass the Godwall, and determines when and where they will reincarnate. It is said that a follower who has led a perfect life in harmony with the [[Corporeal World]] will reincarnate not as an elf, but as a [[Spirits|spirit]]. Reincarnated Elves do not retain memories of their previous lives.

## The Fundamentals
In the Nihir Lawall tradition [[The Fundamentals]] are the creators of the celestial rhythms which created the [[Corporeal World]]. Each god is an aspect of divinity with its own associated cycles and [[Elves#The Mutacurir|mutacurirs]] which must be tended to and harmonized with through ritual. [[Elenwe The Farmer]], as keeper of all cycles, serves as the conductor of the divine rhythms, and serves as the ultimate authority of the interpretation of Nihir Lawall.
