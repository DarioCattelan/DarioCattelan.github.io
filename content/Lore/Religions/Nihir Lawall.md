The Nihir Lawall (nee-HEER la-WHALL), or "the Way of the Cycle" in [[Common]], is the codified religion of the [[Elves]] from [[Elenwe The Farmer]]. It is an [[axiomatist]] animist religion that teaches that the [[Corporeal World]] has a cyclical rhythm to its being that is empowered by regular observation of harmonious rituals. Followers of the religion believe that the souls of [[Elves]] are perpetual, and will reincarnate from beyond [[The Godwall]], and that [[Spirits]] were once elves who had achieved perfect harmony during their life.

# Core Beliefs
1. Repetition brings us closer to divine perfection
2. There exist key moments in the stream of time with great power
3. With age comes wisdom, with wisdom comes divinity
4. The soul is eternal, and will reincarnate in harmony with the last life lived

# Beliefs

## Reincarnation
The faithful of Nihir Lawall hold that [[Elenwe The Farmer]], goddess of cycles, judges the souls of dead [[Elves]] as they pass the Godwall, and determines when and where they will reincarnate. It is said that a follower who has led a perfect life in harmony with the [[Corporeal World]] will reincarnate not as an elf, but as a [[Spirits|spirit]]. Reincarnated Elves do not retain memories of their previous lives.

## The Fundamentals
In the Nihir Lawall tradition [[The Fundamentals]] are the creators of the celestial rhythms which created the [[Corporeal World]]. Each god is an aspect of divinity with its own associated cycles and [[Elves#The Mutacurir|mutacurirs]] which must be tended to and harmonized with through ritual. [[Elenwe The Farmer]], as keeper of all cycles, serves as the conductor of the divine rhythms, and serves as the ultimate authority of the interpretation of Nihir Lawall.
