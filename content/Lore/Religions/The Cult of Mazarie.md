# Overview
The Cult of Mazarie, or "Cult of the Farmer" in [[Common]], is the federated theocracy of the [[Nihir Lawall]]. ^c80ad3

Spiritually, the cult's role is to interpret patterns and auguries of the [[Corporeal World]] as they pertain to the will of [[Elenwe The Farmer|Elenwe]] and determine which [[Elves#Traditions & Beliefs|mutacurir and alnucur]] should be observed at the national, municipal, and familial levels. The Mazaram, priests of the cult, are also the facilitators of the [[Elves#cilaf|cilaf]], serving as the entry point to questions beyond the immediate family.

Additionally, the Cult of Mazarie has an important bureaucratic role in the administration of faith in the Republic. The Mazaram collect tithes for the imperial treasury, and enforce the laws of the High Tari. Most state activities are entwined with religious ritual, and local authorities are often either Mazaram themselves, or else keep Mazaram advisors close at hand.
# Organization
## The Mazaram

The Mazaram are the priests of the Cult of Mazarie. Unlike the [[Elves#The Majilis|Majilis]], the Cult of the Mazarie is not a gerontocracy and its membership is open to any elf. Many senators of the Majilis are themselves Mazaram, integrating the cult deeply into the rule of the state.

There is no strict hegemony in the Cult of Mazarie, and administration of the large, nationalized offices are determined by appointed in the Majilis, or else legal ownership of temples and religiously associated lands where applicable.

## The Mazaram and the Majilis

The [[Elves#The Majilis|Elven Majilis]] is required to yield time to members of the Mazaram during a special session each season to hear the season's required rituals. None but [[Elenwe The Farmer|the High Tari]] is able to dispute the ritual needs of the Cult of Mazarie for the season, and the Majilis are required to provide any resources required for the rituals that are approved by the High Tari. The Majilis cannot break their session or pass to new legislation until the Mazaram yield the floor.
