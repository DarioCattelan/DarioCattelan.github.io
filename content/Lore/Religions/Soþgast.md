The Soþgast (SOOTH-gahst), or simply Soþgast, is the system of believe of the [[Tallmen]] of [[Friðstow]]. It is an animist non-[[axiomatist]] religion that believes the world itself is a powerfully manifested [[Spirits|Spirit]], referred to as [[Gast]], and that all corporeal life, including [[The Fundamentals]], and merely aspects of Gast that have forgotten their origin.

This belief system is highly controversial to followers of [[axiomatist]] religions, as it questions [[The Fundamentals]] right to rule, and establishes [[axiomatic mortals]] as the same tier of being as the gods.

Luckily for [[The Fundamentals]], Soþgast is an all but extinct faith, and is highly esoteric in [[Kaloshen]].

# Core Beliefs
1. All creatures are one being, any harm done to one is harm done to all
2. By forgetting we become less divine, in memory we return to divinity
3. Any Spirit could grow to hold a world, to honor a Spirit is to honor a universe unborn
