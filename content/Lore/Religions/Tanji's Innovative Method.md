Tanji's Innovative Method (TIM) is a "scientific method" used to conceive, test, and log processes, procedures, traditions, ideologies, grudges, and theories into  [[The Codex]]. It is one of the central practices of [[Esoteric Timism]], the ancestral religion [[Dwarves]], and is used ubiquitously to define what can be accepted as "Dwarven Tradition". Adding to [[The Codex]] via TIM requires a lengthy peer review process, which can take up to decades to complete.

Due to its success in the fields of rationalism and analytical experimentation, many non-followers of [[Esoteric Timism]] employ parts of the TIM in their research and study.
