# Overview
Esoteric Timism is a nontheistic nomistic dwarven religion that believes tradition and rationalism is morally superior and should form the basis of law. Esoteric Timism is so named after [[Thastus' Innovative Method]] (TIM), the system by which the faithful evaluate processes, procedures, traditions, ideologies, grudges, and theories. Concepts assessed and approved by the TIM are codified in a central text called [[The Codex]] or sometimes the Codex of TIM.

Followers of Esoteric Timism believe the religion is practiced by [[Dwemeran The Collector]], with its practices deeply tied to their domain of Preservation, though the god is not an explicit object of worship in the Codex itself.
# Core Beliefs
1. Tradition and rationalism is morally superior to deviancy and mysticism
2. An expert opinion is more valuable than a non-expert opinion
3. Laws should be moral and derived from expertise
4. Innovation requires careful evaluation by experts

# Organization
Esoteric Timism is a nomistic religion, meaning its tenets in the Codex are interpreted as legal codes by a theocracy. Esoteric Timism has no central authority for the interpretation of the Codex, and is composed of clerical schools each with their own philosophy. Clerics of the schools debate their interpretations of the Codex as it pertains to ethical and legal conduct. [[Dwarves#Dwemer rule and fiefs|Dwemers]] are the ultimate head of all clerical schools in a fief, and have final interpretation of the Codex as it pertains to governance.

## Schools of Esoteric Timism
Their are two main schools of Esoteric Timism, which themselves have hundreds of sub-schools called cabals.

## Clerics of TIM
Experts in rationalism and the [[Thastus' Innovative Method|TIM]] who serve as spiritual and legal advisers are called Clerics, or sometimes Clerics of TIM. Becoming a cleric require years of study in seminary schools and are well versed in grammar, rhetoric, logic, philosophy (moral and natural), geometry, arithmetic, geology, and theology.

Clerics of TIM serve as judges, law makers, barristers, doctors, astronomers, philosophers; essentially all forms of scholars in the [[Dwarven Fiefs]].

# Rituals
Followers of Esoteric Timism follow one all encompassing set of rituals, those of [[Thastus' Innovative Method]].
