# Overview
The Fundamental Tenets are a the highest laws of [[The Fundamentals]]. They were set upon stone during the first [[Confluence of the Gods]]. The Tenets are universally agreed upon by all the gods, and enforced by their Heralds and clerics throughout the realm.

# The Tenets
1. **The Tenant of Makers:** Thou Shalt Obey Thy Makers And Their Servants
For their word is law, and their wisdom vast.

2. **The Tenant of Spirits:** Thou Shalt Honour The Spirits
For they are an extension of the great creation of the Gods, and are cherished.

3. **The Tenant of the Dead:** Thou Shalt Not Speak To That Which Is Dead
For those that are gone lie in sacred rest, and their words are not for mortals.

4. **The Tenant of Avarice:** Thou Shalt Not Covet the Breath of Others
For Breath is a hallowed gift bestowed upon each of us with purpose and glory by the Gods.

5. **The Tenant of Sowing:** Thou Shalt Sow Thy Breath With Guidance
For it is mightier than a sword and takes no care in its wounding.

# Enforcement of the Tenets
Every culture in Kaloshen has their own interpretation and enforcement of the Tenets.

In the elven tradition, the Tenant of the Dead is interpreted as directly applying to [[The Godwall]] and the [[Fable of the Weaver]]. [[Clan Tatar]] in the [[Elven Province of Yarahn]] bar mortals from travelling to the Godwall, who's borders can be reached by land in the far north.
