# Description

## Preferred Form
A tall traveller in rough sewn black clothing of a highwayman. He wears a tricorn hat with a swallow tail feather, black greatcoat with iron buttons, leather riding pants stressed and worn, black leather gloves, and thick iron cloud stepping shoes. His face is obscured by a mask, and he carries a bone flute in his coat.

# Ideals and Doctrine

# History
At some point in the past, the Nomad betrayed the other [[The Fundamentals|Fundamentals]] in an event called [[The Wæl]]. In response, there was a great battle between the gods. Sometime during the war, [[Elenwe The Farmer]] managed to banish the Nomad to endlessly walk the world, never reaching his destination, and [[Dwemeran The Collector]] stole his name, so that none may ever invite him in for respite.
