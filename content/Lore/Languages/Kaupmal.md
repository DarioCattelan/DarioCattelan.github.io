# Speakers
Kaupmal is spoken by the [[Tankedyr]] of [[the Kaupangsborg]]. It is sometimes used by malevolent, Devil-classified [[Spirits]] as an alternate language in pact making.

# Script
Kaupmal is a relatively simple script to write as it has few letters in its alphabet. Inversely, it can be tricky to read as it relies on numerous accents to modify its limited alphabet. Kaupmal allows much nuance in the conjugation of the imperative, and non-native speakers can be easily confused when following instructions in Kaupmal.

Kaupmal is believed to have been derived from [[Diabolic]], the language of pact-seeking [[Spirits]], and shares a similar, though simplified, linguistic script and syntax.

# Phonics
Kaupmal has long, bright vowels and crisp, clustered consonants. It is a high pitched, tonal language that is easy to whisper and still understand. Poetry and music is often written in Kaupmal can leave the listener with a warm, glowing feeling in their chest and belly.
