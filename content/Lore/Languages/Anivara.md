Anivara is the native language of [[Spirits]] and [[The Fundamentals]]. It is heavily used in historical record and classical education for [[axiomatic mortals]].

# Speakers
[[Spirits]], [[Heralds]], and [[The Fundamentals]] are all beings who regularly converse or write in Anivara. Anivara is a language of impressions, ideas, and concepts, and its syntax does not lend well to fine details.

# Script
Anivara has two forms: Old Anivara and New Anivara. Old Anivara is a pictographic language of hieroglyphs. New Anivara is simplified into glyphs and characters, which can be combined to form words. Anivara characters are often blown up and used as floor plans for gardens, temples, and shrines. Anivara is also often spelled out syllabically in Common.

# Phonics
Anivara is clean, simple, and very consistent in its sounds. It is crisp, steady, and deliberate, with pure and unchanging vowels and consonants. Anivara easily echoes in large spaces.
