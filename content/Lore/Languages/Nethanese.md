Nethanese is a dead language that was once the native language of [[Humans]]. Nethanese was made magically taboo during [[The Wæl]], and knowledge of the language was expunged from the mind of all listeners.

# Speakers
Nobody truly knows how Nethanese was supposed to sound when spoken aloud, but the [[Tallmen]] of [[Friðstow]] study the language in great detail, attempting to learn it once again.

# Script
Nethanese was written in a wedge-shaped script that denote syllabic sounds. The script bears no resemblance to any existing language, and even those who have spent their life learning Nethanese find it difficult to read in a way that suggests their is an [[Arcana|arcane]] effect on the language.

# Phonics
When spoken, Nethanese sounds oddly like [[Common]], but this may be due to the magical nature of Common in combination with the fact that the only speakers of Nethanese are the [[Tallmen]] of [[Friðstow]], for whom Common is their mother tongue.
