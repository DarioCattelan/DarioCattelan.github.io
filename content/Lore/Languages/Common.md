Common is unique among languages in that it magically takes root in the mind of sapient creatures after a few weeks of study, allowing them to read, write, and speak Common as if it was their native language.

# Speakers
Common is now the native tongue of only [[Humans]], but has been adopted as a universal second language by [[axiomatic mortals]], due to its magically adaptable nature.

# Script
Common has a 26 letter alphabet of small circles mixed with easily scribed vertical lines. It is easy to write Common in close, tight script or large, structured pages. All races find the Common script easily legible at a distance of 20 feet, and even beginners can read the script without having to mouth the words aloud.

# Phonics

Common has both long and short vowels, and consonants can produce different sounds depending on their combination with other letters. All [[axiomatic mortals]] find Common vaguely reminiscent of their mother tongue.
