| Common                |                       |
Common is unique among languages in that it magically takes root in the mind of sapient creatures after a few weeks of study, allowing them to read, write, and speak Common as if it was their native language.

# Speakers
All axiomatic mortals. It is the native tongue of [[Humans]], but has been adopted as a universal second language by [[axiomatic mortals]], due to its magically adaptable nature.
