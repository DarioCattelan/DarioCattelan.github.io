# Speakers
Elvish is the language spoken, written, and read by [[Elves]].

# Script
Elvish script is looping, serifed, and symmetrical, and whole words are written without lifting the quill from the page. Elvish script refuses to be carved, engraved, or tattooed on plants or animals.

# Phonics

Elvish is an elegant language of soft consonants and vowels with an almost musical tonality.
