The Three Peoples Army was the force lead by [[Grastus the Free]] during [[The Orlin Civil War]] and [[Mothers' Heresy]] following the [[Edict of Axiomatic Equality]]. During the Heresy, the army was split into two Hordes of approximately equal size, the Northern Horde and Southern Horde.

# The Northern Horde
The Norther Horde conquered much of [[Almam Steppe]] and the [[Ardalidin Region]]. The Northern Horde was eventually defeated by the [[Yoharna Lahai Rasul|Herald of Heroes]] during [[Mothers' Heresy#The Battle of Abu Khasus|the Battle of Abu Khasus]].

# The Southern horde
The Southern Horde swept through [[Orlang]] and [[Audland]], pushing into [[Kusisavana]] up to the [[Okungwulo Desert]]. The Southern Horde was never defeated, though suffered several pyrrhic victories, and eventually decided to settle in an oasis at the heart of modern day [[Tentsuger]].
