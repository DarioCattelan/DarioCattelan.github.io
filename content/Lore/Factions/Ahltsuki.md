## Overview
The Ahltsuki was the [[Primeval bloodlines|primeval bloodline]] of Royal Hobgoblins that lead the [[Orlins|Orlin]] before the [[The Orlin Civil War]]. They were overthrown by [[Grastus the Free]], and their defeat lead to the Orlin adopting a communist socio-economic system.
