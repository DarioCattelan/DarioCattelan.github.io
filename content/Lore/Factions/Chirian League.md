# Overview
 The Chirian League was an alliance of Northern powers in the [[Ardalidin Region]] who unified to oppose the [[Three People's Army]] during [[Mothers' Heresy]]. The forces of the league were commanded by three [[Heralds]] of [[The Fundamentals]]; the [[Yoharna Lahai Rasul|Herald of Heroes]] commanded the army, the [[Reith Habass Rasul|Herald of Tides]] commanded the navy, and [[Oberron Montoire]] maintained overall high command. The Headquarters of the league was in [[Straathûs]] where the leagues combined treasury was held.

The Chirian League suffered several defeats at the hands of the [[Three People's Army]] early in the conflict, but won a definitive victory at the [[Mothers' Heresy#The Battle of Abu Khasus|Battle of Abu Khasus]], leading to the end of the war.
