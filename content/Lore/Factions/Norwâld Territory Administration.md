The Norwâld Territory Administration (NTA) is a guild of rangers and scouts who administrate the [[Norwâld |Norwald Reserve]] . The NTA was established in the year (TBD) by [[The Norwâld Contract]] between the [[Elven Republic]] and the Merchant Council of [[Straathûs]] which dictates the rules and regulations by which the guild operates.

The Norwâld Territory Administration serves to monitor and intercept poachers, enforce the political borders of the [[Norwâld]] contract, prevent ecocide, monitor for natural disasters and corrupt [[spirits]], monitor and deter encroachment of foreign powers from the [[Almam Steppe]], and protect and collect tariffs from travellers and traders passing through the reserve.

# Base of Operations
The base of operations of the NTA is located at the southernmost point of the [[Norwâld]] border, and is accessible from [[Straathûs]] by boat through a series of rivers and waterways. The base serves a dual purpose as both a trading post and center of commerce for the region, and as the home of the Tankedyr that comprise the main ranks of the organization, including the office of the Tollmaster, and the two ranger mercenary companies, [[The Wâldûlen]] and [[The Waldbist]].

# History

## Founding
Before the establishment of the Territory Administration, the forest of [[Norwâld]] was a vast, verdant, and wild forest that stretched from the northern [[Jabal Mountains]], to the southern lakes of the [[Ardalidin Region]] and [[Netham]], and was bounded by the [[Almam Steppe]] to the east. About 2% of the forest was destroyed by Orlin forces during [[Mothers' Heresy]], prompting the [[Elven Republic]] to seek additional assistance with safeguarding their claim on the region. Finding a willing ally in the [[Tankedyr]] of [[Straathûs]], the Merchant Council penned [[The Norwâld Contract|The Norwâld Charter]], allowing the city state effective land rights of the [[Norwâld]] forest.

[[The Norwâld Contract|The Norwâld Charter]] established a "Forest Law" and an organizing guild, the Norwâld Territory Administration. The guild would serve to regulate and manage trade between the [[Elven Republic]] and [[The Orlin Communes]], expanding trading operations to the mid-north for [[the Kaupangsborg]], as well as supplying the Republic with an intermediary buffer of [[Tankedyr]] mercenaries should the Orlin seek to expand further into their territory. As it stands, the current political situation provides the clearest advantage of this contract to the [[Tankedyr]], whose leaders do not believe a new invasion from the east to be likely, and who consider the Territory Administration just the first step towards establishing a new, more permanent Kaupangsborg city state in the region.

## Defense of Norwald and the Waldbist
The very first Warden appointed to the post of Territory Warden was [[Lyuk Fenorson]], a well known mercenary captain from [[the Kaupangsborg]]. During a series of raids by [[Orlins|Orlin warriors]] in 59 [[Era of the Five|EF]],  Lyuk's forces gained renown in the north for their successful defense of [[Norwâld]] while greatly outnumbered. Considered in a league of their own, this mercenary group formed the first team of rangers, known as [[The Waldbist|The Wâldbist]]. 

## Disappearance of Lyuk
The activities of the Waldbist during the early years of the NTA are not a matter of public record, and it came as a great shock to public of [[the Kaupangsborg]] when the Merchant Council of [[Straathûs]] revealed that [[Lyuk Fenorson]] and the majority of [[The Waldbist]] had disappeared only a year after their heroic defense of Norwald. Only one member of the original Waldbist, Fenna Freydottir, was accounted for, and the reason for the disappearance of the Waldbist has never been revealed to the public.

## Reorganization of the NTA
In 61 [[Era of the Five|EF]] a new Territory Warden was selected by the High Merchant Council of [[Straathûs]], with some suspicion that the new incumbent Wolter was the personal choice of the [[Truhuggun#The Chantry of Truhuggun|the Chantry of Truhuggun]]. The Wâldbist were soon stood up again following this crisis under the command of Tjark Heldirsson. The role of nighwatch versus daywatch of the reserve was split into separate companies, and a new company dubbed [[The Wâldûlen]] were established under the command of Fenna Freydottir.
