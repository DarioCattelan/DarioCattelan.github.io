# Overview

![[Norwald Hierarchy.png\|hsmall]]
[[Norwald Hierarchy.png\|Full Quality Norwâld Hierarchy]]

The Norwâld Territory Administration was created in (Year TBD) by a contract between the Elven Republic and the High Merchant Council of [[Straathûs]] concerning the administration of a National-park type nature reserve referred to as the [[Norwâld]] forest or reserve. This contract is legally known as "A contract between the Elven Republic and Kaupangsborg of Straathûs regarding the administration of strategic territory and trade facilitation for the benefit of sovereign nations in relative entente" and informally referred to as "The Norwâld Contract" or "The Norwâld Charter" depending on if the speaker is Tankedyr or the Elvish respectively. 

The base of operations is located at the southernmost point of the [[Norwâld]] border, accessible from [[Straathûs]] by boat from the large lake which itself connects to the ocean and the Kaupangsborg. The base serves a dual purpose as both a trading post and centre of commerce for the region, and as the home of the Tankedyr that comprise the main ranks of the organization, including the office of the Tollmaster, and the two ranger mercenary companies, [[The Wâldûlen]] and the Wâldbist.

As a team, the Norwâld Territory Administration serves to monitor and intercept poachers, enforce the political borders of the [[Norwâld]] contract, prevent ecocide, monitor for natural disasters and corrupt [[spirits]], especially monitor for signs of encroachment from the Almam Steppe and protect and collect tariffs from travellers and traders passing through the reserve.

# Background

Before the establishment of the Territory Administration, the forest of [[Norwâld]] was a vast, verdant and wild forest that stretched from the Elven mountains to the north, down to the lakes of the Ardalidin region and [[Netham]] to the south, and ended as the landscape transformed into the [[Almam Steppe]] to the east. About 2% of the forest was destroyed by Orlin forces who waged a war against the Elves in (Year TBD), prompting the [[Elves]] to see additional assistance with safeguarding their claim on the territory. Finding a willing ally in the [[Tankedyr]], a contract was drawn up that allowed the [[Tankedyr]] effective land rights of the [[Norwâld]] forest, for as long as relations between the [[Tankedyr]] and [[Elves]] remained in both party's best interests. 

The High Merchant Council of the [[Tankedyr]], always looking to expand their territory, quickly set up a contract regarding "Forest Law" and commissioned the establishment of a base of operations, the Norwâld Territory Administration. This outpost would serve a few factors, regulating and managing trade between the [[Elves]] and [[Halflings]], and expanding trading operations to the mid-north for the Tankedyr, and supplying the [[Elves]] with an advance warning buffer of [[Tankedyr]] rangers should the Orlin seek to expand further into their territory. As it stands, the current political situation provides the clearest advantage of this contract to the [[Tankedyr]], whose leaders do not believe a new invasion from the east to be likely, and who consider the Territory Administration just the first step towards establishing a new, more permanent Kaupangsborg in the region.

The territory administration is known throughout the land, mostly for their invention of an organised cadre of "Forest Rangers"; mercenary-like bands of Tankedyr with survival, scouting, and combat training that monitor all trails and roads near the outpost to ensure the flow of trade continues with as little resistance as possible. The outpost's strategic location on the banks of Ardalidin's largest lake provide boat access from [[Straathûs]] by way of Bighollow, and the waterways remain the most efficient way of travelling around the interior of [[Norwâld]], although establishing walkable trails is one of the duties of the Territory Rangers employed by the administration.

The Territory Administration is only a few decades old during the current setting, and has not gone without intrigue and conflict of its own. The very first Warden appointed to the post was the folk-hero-like figure **Lyuk**, a well known [[Tankedyr]] Grey Wolf whose band of mercenaries repelled Orlin forces in [[Norwâld]] during the last significant conflict. Considered in a league of their own, this mercenary group formed the first team of rangers, known as the Wâldbist. Much of what they accomplished during the first few years of the Territory Administration's creation is not very well known outside of those who were there, and [[Tankedyr]] society was shocked by the news of the Wâldbist's disappearance only 10 years ago from the current setting. 

A new Territory Warden was quickly selected by the High Merchant Council of [[Straathûs]], with some suspicion that the new incumbent **Wolter** was the personal choice of the [[Gaodheimr]] leadership. Only one ranger remains with the administration who ever personally saw **Lyuk**, Fenna, the current leader of [[The Wâldûlen]], who was the youngest and newest member of the Wâldbist before all other members of the team dissipated. 

The Wâldbist were soon stood up again following this crisis, and Fenna remained with the Territory Administration, on her own terms and with her own team of owls known as [[The Wâldûlen]]. Fenna does not speak very much of what she saw or knows of the disappearance of **Lyuk**.
# Additional Details

**Territory Warden Wolter**
A black bear Tankedyr who would very much prefer to have remained in [[Straathûs]]. Was widely considered a diplomatic political appointment to the role of Territory Warden as a check on the rising ambitions of [[The Wâldûlen]] Head Ranger Fenna, who many expected would have filled the vacant position after the previous Warden disappeared. Some say Wolter had the specific endorsement of the [[Goadheimr]] leadership, which carries much weight in [[Tankedyr]] society. To those that work in the Administration, Wolter has a reputation of being more of a politician than a Warden.
