# Overview

![[Norwald Hierarchy.png\|hsmall]]
[[Norwald Hierarchy.png\|Full Quality Norwâld Hierarchy]]

The Norwâld Territory Administration was created in (Year TBD) by a contract between the Elven Republic and the High Merchant Council of [[Straathûs]] concerning the administration of a National-park type nature reserve referred to as the [[Norwâld]] forest or reserve. This contract is legally known as "A contract between the Elven Republic and Kaupangsborg of Straathûs regarding the administration of strategic territory and trade facilitation for the benefit of sovereign nations in relative entente" and inormally reffered to as "The Norwâld Contract" or "The Norwâld Charter" depending on if the speaker is Tankedyr or the Elvish respecitvely. 

The base of operations is located at the southernmost point of the [[Norwâld]] border, accessible from [[Straathûs]] by boat from the large lake which itself connects to the ocean and the Kaupangsborg. The base serves a dual purpose as both a trading post and centre of commerce for the region, and as the home of the Tankedyr that comprise the main ranks of the organization, including the office of the Tollmaster Jakip, and the two ranger mercenary companies, [[The Wâldûlen]] and the Wâldbist.

As a team, the Norwâld Territory Administration serves to monitor and intercept poachers, enforce the political borders of the [[Norwâld]] contract, prevent ecocide, monitor for natural disasters and corrupt [[spirits]], especially monitor for signs of encroachment from the Almam Steppe and protect and collect tariffs from travellers and traders passing through the reserve.
