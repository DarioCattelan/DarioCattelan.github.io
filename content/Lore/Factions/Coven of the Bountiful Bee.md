# Overview
The Coven of the Bountiful Bee is a [[Witches Coven]] founded by [[Orisin]] and their familiar [[Jobachi]] during the beginning of the [[Age of Orlin]]. The coven aims to regulate the use of [[Alchemy]] such that the [[Arcana]] of the world remains in balance, and to protect [[Spirits]] who are impacted by the production of alchemical products. The coven is the originator of many [[Alchemical Guilds]].
