# Overview
The Barafu were a [[Primeval bloodlines|Primeval Bloodline]] of the [[Ice People]] who crossed into [[Maji's Mountains]] during [[Trial of Maji|The Vault]]. They were said to be descended from a race of [[Demigods]] and had an innate ability to wield [[The Breath of the World]].

The Barafu are believed to be responsible for releasing [[Forgotten Beasts]] onto the land during the [[Age of Spirits]]. The destruction wrought by the [[Forgotten Beasts]] left the Barafu all but wiped out. Legend holds that the four surviving members of the Barafu founded the [[Four Primeval Dynasties]].
