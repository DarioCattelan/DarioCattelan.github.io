# Overview
The Igazichumvi dynasty is a [[Primeval bloodlines|Primeval Bloodline]] of the [[Dwarves]] that rules the [[Sum Siebea]] fief. They are one of the [[Four Primeval Dynasties]] said to be descended from the mythical [[Barafu]] Tribe. Their current leader is [[Dwemer Falakhe Igazichumvi]].
