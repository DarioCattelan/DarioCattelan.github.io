
The Chivalric Orders of the Fey are (NUMBER) arts of chivalry that are learned by members of the [[Fey Folk#Chivalric Hierarchy|Chivalric Nobility]] in [[Hoshi Hall]]. Each art has a series of ranks denoting the ability of its members, and the orders serve as guild or faction within the fey nobility. Masters of the Orders are some of the Nobles who are the most reliably in favor with [[Titania The Muse]], as they train prospective knights in the arts, churning out a constant supply of novel toys to the Summer Queen. Students of an order are called Lexperts (lex-PAIRS).

# The Order of the Winking Willow

>[!NOTE] Pathfinder 2e equivalent class
> Ranger

The Order of the Winking Willow is a Chivalric Order of the Fey whose martial arts focus on the skills of tracking and ambushing foes with all manner of weaponry. They are skilled masters of the field, practicing the arts of hedge knightery and errantry, and are reliable outdoorsmen. Lexperts of the Winking Willow often use their skills to protect the green places of the world, and typically have excellent connections with [[Spirits#Zuhachi|Zuhachi]] and [[Spirits#Leshi|Leshi]].

# The Order of the King's Valets


> [!NOTE] Pathfinder 2e equivalent class
> Champion, Guardian

The Order of the King's Valets is a Chivalric Order of the Fey whose martial arts focus on the protection of allies and companions. They are the gentlemen entourage of the Kings and Heralds of the [[Fey Folk#Heralds the Oberons|Fey Folk, Oberon]], and often serve as their attendants on adventures. Devotion in a Lexpert of the King's Valets often earns one [[The Breath of Devotion]] from [[Titania The Muse]] and her [[Heralds|Herald]]. Courage, valor, and integrity are words often associated with King's Valets, and they are highly respected in the nobility.

# The Order of the Slumbering Heart

> [!NOTE] Pathfinder 2e equivalent class
> Animist, Summoner, Witch

The Order of the Slumbering Heart is a Chivalric Order of the Fey who's membership is composed of [[Fey Folk#Heart's companions|Heart's Companions]], faeries who have attracted a bond with a  [[Spirits]] by swearing to a Virtue.  As Heart's Companions, Lexperts are often spiritual leaders and guides to other Fey Folk. The martial arts of the Slumbering Heart involve leveraging the power of one's boosted [[Breath]] to cast magics via a variety of [[Breath#Methods of Breath|methods of Breath]].
