The Suutin is the catchall name for the sedentary [[Orlins|Orlin]] tribes that populate the [[Almam Steppe]], [[Orlin Plains]], and the [[Southern Steppe]]. The Suutin have long held institutional governance over the Olon since the reign of the [[Bauyokhan Empire]] in the [[Age of Orlin]], but have slowly been losing their monopoly on power as Olon tribal members are elected to committees of [[The Orlin Communes]].

# Unification with the Olon
Prior to [[Mothers' Heresy]], [[The Olon]] and the Suutin were a segregated people within different Links of [[The Ordning]]. In the 70 years since the Heresy, the Orlin Council of Unity has proffered decades of social programs to integrate the Olon into the permanent hives of the Suutin.
