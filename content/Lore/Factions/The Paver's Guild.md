# Overview
The Paver's Guild is a crafts guild of [[the Kaupangsborg]] who's primary business is the construction and maintenance of public works and infrastructure. The Paver's Guild is the current holder of the contract to maintain [[The Grand Highway]] between [[Karavanskali]] and [[Gainiv Halias]], as well as the [[Orken Pass]] between [[Rasteplads]] and [[Hobbsborg]].

# Organization
The Paver's Guild has a typical organizational hierarchy of Apprentices, Journeymen, Master Craftsmen, and Guild Masters.

| Position         | Powers and Responsibilities                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Guild Master     | Represents the Guild in a city. Acquires and negotiates contracts, organizes the affairs of the Guild. Paid from tithes to the Guild.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Master Craftsman | Runs a guild owned workshop. In cities with reduced Guild presence, the Master Craftsmen is also the Guild Master. Instructs journeymen and does only the finest and most complex finishing work. Paid on commission, receiving commissions from the Guild Master. Pays tithes to the Guild on each commission. |
| Journeyman       | Skilled laborer of a workshop. Instructs apprentices and learns directly from the Master Craftsman. Paid a portion of the commission directly from the Master Craftsman. Pays for own room and board.                                                                                                           |
| Apprentice       | Trainee of a workshop, doing the unskilled labor. Learns from journeymen. Typically unpaid, but housed and fed by the workshop.                                                                                                                                                                                 |


## Headquarters
The primary guildhall of the Paver's Guild is in [[Rasteplads]], with major guildhalls also in [[Karavanskali]] and [[Gainiv Halias]], though it is not uncommon to find minor offices of the Paver's Guild in the headquarters of other similar guilds across [[the Kaupangsborg]].
