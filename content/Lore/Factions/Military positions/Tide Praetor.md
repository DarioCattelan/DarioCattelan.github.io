The Tide Praetor is the highest rank in the [[Elven Republic]]'s navy. In theory, is a political position appointed by the [[Elves#The Majilis|The Majilis]], however for the past 239 years the position been continually occupied by the [[Reith Habass Rasul|Herald of Tides]].

The Tide Praetor serves as the commander in chief of all fleets in the [[Elven Republic]] and its provinces. The Tide Praetor also holds the powers of magistrate over the navy, granting them  complete legislative and judiciary authority over members of the elven navy while they are serving.
