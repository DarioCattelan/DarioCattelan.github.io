# Overview
The Summer Company is the legion of veteran soldiers commanded by the [[Yoharna Lahai Rasul|Herald of Heroes]] during times of crisis. The Summer Company is an elite company of ~1500 to 2000 warriors, mages, and support staff that is formed and disbanded to address specific crises. Most recently, the Summer Company defended the [[Elven Republic]] during [[Mothers' Heresy]], and defeated the Northern Horde of the [[Three People's Army]] at [[Mothers' Heresy#The Battle of Abu Khasus|the Battle of Abu Khasus]].

The Summer Company's reputation is extraordinary across [[Kaloshen]] has a moral army, that does not engage in pillaging and wanton destruction.
