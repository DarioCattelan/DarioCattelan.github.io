The Four Primeval Dynasties are the four [[Primeval bloodlines]] of the [[Dwarves]] founded during the [[Age of Spirits]] after the death of the [[Barafu]]. The original Primeval Dynasties are:

- [[Igazidrako Dynasty]]
- [[Igazichumvi Dynasty]]
- [[Inlizi Kharambo Dynasty]]
- [[Igazijiwe Dynasty]]
