# Overview
 The Godsway Trade Alliance was an alliance of Southern powers in [[Audland]], the [[Orlin Plains]], and the [[Southern Steppe]] who unified to oppose the [[Three People's Army]] during [[Mothers' Heresy]]. The forces of the alliance were commanded by the [[Dwemer Falakhe]] of [[Sum Siebea]], and supported by mercenary armies purchased by the Pashas of [[Rasteplads]] and [[Karavanskali]]. The Headquarters of the Alliance was in [[Sum Siebea]] where the leagues combined treasury was held.
