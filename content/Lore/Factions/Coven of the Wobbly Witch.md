# Overview
The Coven of the Wobbly Witch is a [[Witches Coven]] founded during the [[Age of Witches]] by [[Shrall the Drunk]]. In its modern incarnation, the coven is dedicated to giving comfort to Spirits who have been forcibly [[Spirits#Alcohol Binding|Bound]] to the [[Corporeal World]].

# History
## Founding and Initial Age
The Coven of the Wobbly Witch was originally founded by [[Shrall the Drunk]] during their journeys following [[The Ritus Ieiunii|The Rite of Fasting]]. Throughout the [[Age of Witches]] and into the [[Age of the Pillar]] the coven's primary mission was to bind and imprison dangerous spirits. This service was not offered free of charge, and by the [[Age of Orlin]] the coven had amassed a huge amount of wealth.

## Money Lending
By the [[Age of Orlin]] the coven had become extremely wealthy and had begun earning greater income from money lending and mercenary work than classical "witchy" services. At the height of their power the coven was said to own major debts from all [[The Fundamentals]], with a rumored 60% of the treasury of [[Old Netham]] being owed to the elders of the coven.

## Disbanding
In 211 [[Era of the Five|ES]] [[Age of the Pillar]] the coven came under direct attack by the leaders of [[Old Netham]]. behind closed doors, [[The Blasphemer]] was said to have met with the [[Heralds]] of the gods, and agreed that the debts of the coven were irredeemable. Accompanied by the [[Hevlon Ibara Rasul|Herald of Disaster]] and [[The Gadian Ri]], [[The Blasphemer]] forcibly disbanded the Coven of the Wobbly Witch. Although the precious metals were returned to the vaults of the gods, [[Graedor The Merchant]] gave many of the physical assets to [[Tankedyr]] merchants, eventually leading to the rise of [[the Kaupangsborg]].

## Refounding
Following [[The Wæl]] at the beginning of the [[Age of the Five|Age of the Diaspora]], many [[Spirits]] were bound in distressing circumstances across [[Kaloshen]]. [[Hannu Egilson]], a young [[Tankedyr]] boy orphaned during the Wael, convinced a small cadre of adventurers to refound the Coven of the Wobbly Witch. In this reformation, the coven's primary goal would not be to bind dangerous spirits, but would instead adopt the vision of its original founder[[Shrall the Drunk]], that is, to give comfort and aid to spirits unwillingly bound.
