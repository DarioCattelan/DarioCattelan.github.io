# Overview
Oberon is the title of the [[Heralds]] and Prince/Princess consorts of [[Titania The Muse]]. [[Titania The Muse|The Empress]] is fickle, her desires ever changing, and thus the number and identity of the Oberons is in constant flux. Male or female, noble or peasant, one or many, [[Titania The Muse|Titania]] appoints and removes Oberon's as she wishes. When more than one Oberon is in vogue, [[Titania The Muse|Titania]] will usually append a moniker to the title, naming them "Oberon the Tall" or "Oberon the Artist" or "Oberon the Loud Sneezer". The Summer Queen has often said she does this for others, as she can "always tell" which one *she* means.
# Heraldic Duties and Powers
Oberon is treated by [[Hoshi Hall]] as if they were a true regent. They declare new laws, hold royal court, lead armies, and make lavish use of the treasury. Oberon is a title that grants ultimate power in [[Hoshi Hall]] second only to [[Titania The Muse|Titania]] herself, and even the nobility play along, hoping no doubt for their turn.

The title of Oberon also comes with a divine mandate: to add novelty to the world. Oberon's have heralded the death of monarchs, been the muse to inventors and artists, manned expeditions of the reaches of the world, and other no doubt lesser know day to day changes. Leaving [[Hoshi Hall]] on a self-imposed exile to change the world is in fact a relatively reliable way to hang on to the title of Oberon, as Titania has been less likely to remove an Oberon when they aren't top of mind.
